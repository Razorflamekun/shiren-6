Postgame dungeon where the player begins the dungeon with a Peach Club equipped.<br/>Peach Club is a weapon that has a chance to transform a defeated monster into a Peach Bun.<br/>Eating a Peach Bun morphs Shiren into the corresponding monster, allowing usage of its abilities.<br/>(Example: Eating a Pumphantasm Bun allows Shiren to traverse wall tiles)

# Strategy

<h3 id="dungeon-characteristics" class="heading3Yellow">Dungeon Characteristics</h3>

- This is one of the harder dungeons, despite the fancy aesthetic and lighthearted story.
    - The in-game clear rate statistic rivals Yamakagashi Pass and Heart of Serpentcoil Island.
- Peach Buns are easy to obtain so hunger is often not an issue, but wind blows early (900 turns).
    - This means you can't linger and farm experience points or items for a very long time.
- The dungeon's finer details seem to be primarily balanced around Peach Buns.
    - Special Monster Houses can appear from 1F, which also includes reading a Monstercall Scroll.
    - The only cannon that gets generated is the cannon which transforms a target into a Peach Bun.
    - Traps which target food or monsters are common, weapons and shields with innate runes are rare.
    - Gitan bags are lower in value, so strategies involving thrown Gitan are less reliable.
- It's difficult to clear this dungeon without learning at least the basics of Peach Bun mechanics.
    - It's much easier once you learn the useful Peach Buns and how foes interact with Morphed status.
    - Certain Peach Buns can be used to gain positive status conditions, or to farm items, etc.
    - A Peach Bun can also be thrown to transform a stronger foe into a weaker monster.
- Shops can be generated, and Peach Buns can appear as merchandise.
    - Merchandise can include powerful Peach Buns that are otherwise unobtainable in the dungeon.
- Peach Buns can also be found in wall tiles, on item islands, and as drops by defeated Chows.
    - The chance for said souces to generate a Peach Bun is quite high, especially sparkling walls.
    - These sources include Peach Buns that don't correlate to the current floor's monster table.

<h3 id="general-advice" class="heading3Yellow">General Advice</h3>

- The initial monster spawn count at the start of each floor is low.
    - It often feels surprisingly manageable even if there are scary monsters in the table.
- Hunger isn't a problem, so linger until the wind blows on early game floors.
- Nigiri Baby (2-4F) can be used to increase Max Fullness and activate Sumo status if desired.
    - Once Sumo status is active, it can usually be maintained just by eating Peach Buns.
- Break-Off Blade and Break-Off Shield can be used without degrading during Morphed status.
    - This is because equipment runes are nullified during Morphed status.
- Throw a Peach Bun at a monster or NPC to transform them into the corresponding monster.
    - If you carry weak Peach Buns like Mamel Bun, you can instantly weaken a tough opponent.
    - A way to steal from a shop is to read Wall-less Scroll → throw a Peach Bun at the Shopkeeper.
- Crossbowboy (5-7F) or Porky (8F) buns can be used to collect lots of arrows or Porky's Rocks.
- Collect Peach Buns of swift monsters like Death Reaper (2-4F) to have an easier time.
    - Swift Peach Buns are also great for escaping from Monster Houses.

<h4 id="morphed-status" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/monsters_s/1-1.png"/> Morphed Status</h4>

- Shiren's stats (HP, attack, and defense) remain the same during Morphed status.
    - In other words, eating a Mamel Bun won't set your stats to be the same as a Mamel.
- Equipment runes and bracelet effects are nullified during Morphed status.
    - Attack and defense increases from your equipped weapon and shield remain in effect.
- Reverting from Morphed status doesn't end the player's turn.
- All status conditions are cured when Shiren eats a Peach Bun or ends Morphed status.
    - This includes both positive and negative status conditions.

<h3 id="important-items-(first-clear)" class="heading3Purple">Important Items (First Clear)</h3>

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/weapon/38.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/33.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>Break-Off Blade, Break-Off Shield</u></b><br/>Easily the best equipment for the first clear when you're not aiming for Peach Bun drops.<br/>The Dulling rune is nullified during Morphed status, so it can be used without upgrade value loss.<br/>Create a Removal Trap using Karakuroid Bun before equipping it for the first time in case it's cursed.</p>

<p><b><u>Peach Club</u></b><br/>Fighting monsters with this equipped is the primary method of obtaining Peach Buns.<br/>It's not the strongest weapon around, so synthesize status inflicting runes using Mixers if possible.<br/>(<u>Blinding Grass</u> → Blinding / <u>Confusion Grass</u> → Confusing / <u>Sedating Grass</u> → Sedating)</p>

<p><b><u>Axe of the Minotaur</u></b><br/>Occasionally dropped by Minotaur (16-24F), and higher base value than a Peach Club.<br/>The Critical rune makes it hard to control Explochin (20-22F)'s HP, but is otherwise fantastic.</p>

<p><b><u>Wolfshead</u></b><br/>Secondary shield for when you're not using Break-Off Shield + Morphed status.</p>

<p><b><u>Watchful Shield, Counter Shield</u></b><br/>Low base value, but viable if progressing with Peach Club while Sumo status is active.<br/>If you also find a Wolfshead, synthesize these as runes instead of maining a weaker shield.</p>

<h4 id="bracelets-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/bracelet.png"/> Bracelets</h4>

<p><b><u>Monster-find Bracelet</u></b><br/>Incredibly useful bracelet that reveals enemy locations on the map.<br/>However, keep in mind that bracelet effects are nullified during Morphed status.</p>

<p><b><u>Item-find Bracelet</u></b><br/>Lets you easily locate items that are buried in wall tiles.<br/>Also helpful for planning a more efficient route when exploring a floor for items.</p>

<p><b><u>Rapid-fire Bracelet</u></b><br/>Boosts projectile accuracy (84% → 91%), and lets you shoot 2 projectiles in 1 turn.<br/>Projectiles are trivial to collect in this dungeon, making this bracelet extremely powerful.<br/>If you find it early on, be sure to linger on Crossbowboy (5-7F) or Karakuroid (4-6F) floors.</p>

<h4 id="projectiles-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/arrow.png"/> <img src="../images/other/rock.png"/> Projectiles</h4>

<p><b><u>Wooden Arrow, Iron Arrow</u></b><br/>Eat a Bowboy or Crossbowboy bun or use an Evasive Incense to collect these easily.<br/>As far as how many to collect, 2 stacks of arrows should be plenty for the first clear.</p>

<p><b><u>Poison Arrow</u></b><br/>Useful when exploring without Morphed status, or when fighting a higher level monster.<br/>Eat a Karakuroid Bun → create Poison Arrow Traps along walls → throw items onto the traps.</p>

<p><b><u>Porky's Rock</u></b><br/>Porky's Rock one-shots a Ghost Samurai (7-12F) or Behemoth Monster (22-24F).<br/>Regular Rocks can also be used, but Porky's Rocks are easier to collect.</p>

<h4 id="grass-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/grass.png"/> Grass</h4>

<p><b><u>Revival Grass, Invincible Grass</u></b><br/>Revival Grass is still easier to use as a revival option than Masked Samurai Bun.<br/>Do your best to be pretty conservative when it comes to consuming these 2 items.</p>

<p><b><u>Sedating Grass, Confusion Grass, Blinding Grass</u></b><br/>Synthesize into a weapon to add status inflicting runes, making non-Morphed status fights easier.</p>

<h4 id="scrolls-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> Scrolls</h4>

<p><b><u>Jitters Scroll, Onigiri Scroll</u></b><br/>Synthesize into a shield to add payback runes, making non-Morphed status fights easier.</p>

<p><b><u>Heavenly Scroll, Earthly Scroll</u></b><br/>Surprisingly common, making it more viable to upgrade gear even with bad Mixer spawn luck.<br/>Some players even skip collecting gear with positive upgrade value as synthesis ingredients.</p>

<p><b><u>Blank Scroll</u></b><br/>Monster Houses can be ruthless in this dungeon, making this scroll a key counter option.<br/>Select <span class="blueText">Write</span> to check the scroll's blessed / cursed state, and don't discard it even if it's cursed.</p>

<h4 id="staves-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> Staves</h4>

<p><b><u>Earthmound Staff</u></b><br/>Can be used to cross water tiles, ensure safety when collecting projectiles,<br/>or to have a one-sided fight when combined with certain Peach Bun abilities.</p>

<p><b><u>Disguising Staff</u></b><br/>Create a high level monster and hunt it to level up during early game.<br/>Fortune Staff can't be found in this dungeon, so don't hesitate to use charges for this purpose.</p>

<p><b><u>Peach Staff</u></b><br/>Rare and powerful staff that transforms a target hit by the magic bullet into a Peach Bun.<br/>Consider swinging it at Mixers to obtain Mixer Buns, which ensure safe synthesis.</p>

<h4 id="pots-&-incenses-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/pot.png"/> Pots & Incenses</h4>

<p><b><u>Preservation Pot</u></b><br/>Extremely important, as it lets you protect and carry more Peach Buns, along with other items.<br/>If you encounter a Bouncy Wall Room, strongly consider duplicating Preservation Pots.</p>

<p><b><u>Blurry Incense</u></b><br/>Always found identified, and can be used to counter a single room Monster House.</p>

<p><b><u>Evasive Incense</u></b><br/>Simply lighting this incense lets you collect arrows or Porky's Rocks from monsters.<br/>Also nice to have for Porko (24-25F) and Pesky Kappa (24-25F) floors.</p>

<h4 id="peach-buns-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/momoman.png"/> Peach Buns</h4>

Eating any non-rotten Peach Bun replenishes 25 fullness and cures all status conditions.<br/>Peach Buns and Onigiri will rot if you enter water after eating an Aquatic type's Peach Bun.

<p><b><u>Rotten Peach Bun</u></b><br/>Obtained by entering water after eating a Spearfish Bun, stepping on a Rotting Trap, etc.<br/>Inflicts 5 damage + a bad status when thrown, which can be used to create and hunt a Cave Mamel.<br/>Possible status conditions include Weakened, Confused, Asleep, Blind, or Deluded status.</p>

<h3 id="important-items-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Blue">Important Items (Sacred Tree Extension)</h3>

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/weapon/24.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/4.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>Peach Club</u></b><br/>Starting item, but additional copies can be found within the dungeon.<br/>Beware of Battler (66-79F) of course, and Mudster & Mudder (47-54F) if you're using it as a rune.<br/>Only 2 rune slots by default, but sacred versions found in shops can have more slots.</p>

<p><b><u>Break-Off Blade, Break-Off Shield</u></b><br/>Still usually the strongest option for trading normal attacks during Morphed status.<br/>It's generally not worth synthesizing runes into this set.</p>

<p><b><u>Axe of the Minotaur</u></b><br/>Occasionally dropped by Minotaur (16-24F) or Megataur (66-79F).<br/>Linger for this weapon drop if you plan on focusing on a mainly non-Morphed status playstyle.<br/>Keep a 2nd copy as a backup in case you lose the Critical rune on Mudster & Mudder (47-54F) floors,<br/>and a Mixer Bun to synthesize it if you're not maining the axe (Mixers don't appear past 30F).</p>

<p><b><u>Regular Shield (Wolfshead, Fuuma Shield, etc.)</u></b><br/>Work on upgrading a basic shield instead of only relying on Break-Off Shield + Morphed status.<br/>Wolfshead or better is ideal, but it's fine to go with a weaker shield if you want more rune slots.<br/>However, keep in mind that the shield rune selection is limited in this dungeon.</p>

<h4 id="bracelets-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/bracelet.png"/> Bracelets</h4>

<p><b><u>Monster-find Bracelet, Item-find Bracelet</u></b><br/>Monster-find Bracelet is nice for dark hallway floors (11-12F, 14-29F, 51-69F).<br/>Item-find Bracelet lets you locate shops and items buried in wall tiles at a glance.</p>

<p><b><u>Rapid-fire Bracelet</u></b><br/>Boosts projectile accuracy (84% → 91%), and lets you shoot 2 projectiles in 1 turn.<br/>Dark hallways are limited to select floor ranges, and projectiles are easy to obtain,<br/>making this bracelet extremely useful - especially if you collect Poison Arrows.</p>

<p><b><u>Cleansing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Counters Terror Scorpion (51-59F), and also helps against Sleep Radish (47-50F).<br/>Not as essential, since you can also counter scorpions by eating a Scorpion family Peach Bun.<br/>Also nice for Behemoth Sleep Radish & Behemoth Frenzy Radish (51-54F, 94-98F) floors.</p>

<p><b><u>Rousing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Counters Sleep Radish (47-50F) and Behemoth Sleep Radish (51-54F, 94-98F).<br/>Nullifies Skull Lord (66-79F)'s Asleep status inflicting magic bullet effect.</p>

<h4 id="projectiles-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/arrow.png"/> <img src="../images/other/rock.png"/> Projectiles</h4>

<p><b><u>Iron Arrow, Poison Arrow</u></b><br/>Use these to conserve better items, or to weaken a foe before finishing them off with Peach Club.<br/>Collect at least 2 stacks, and keep a Baby Tank or Karakuroid bun on hand to restock later on.</p>

<p><b><u>Porky's Rock</u></b><br/>Porky's Rock one-shots a Ghost Samurai (7-12F) or Behemoth Monster (22-24F, 31-39F, 51-54F, 94-98F).<br/>That said, avoid getting in range of B-Sleep Radish and B-Frenzy Radish (51-54F, 94-98F).<br/>Collect 1 stack on Porky (8F) or Porko (24-27F) floors.</p>

<h4 id="staves-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> Staves</h4>

<p><b><u>Narrow-escape Staff, Guiding Staff</u></b><br/>Pair with Monster-find Bracelet to quickly locate the stairs on dangerous floors.</p>

<p><b><u>Peach Staff</u></b><br/>Rare and powerful staff that transforms a target hit by the magic bullet into a Peach Bun.<br/>Swing it at a valuable monster like Mixer when you want to guarantee obtaining a Peach Bun.</p>

<p><b><u>Paralyzing Staff, Sealing Staff</u></b><br/>Single-target options for disabling threats that have a dangerous special attack.<br/>Examples: Polygon Singa (31-39F), Sleep Radish (47-50F), Nigiri Boss (60-65F), Skull Lord (66-79F), etc.</p>

<h4 id="scrolls-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> Scrolls</h4>

<p><b><u>Confusion Scroll, Slumber Scroll</u></b><br/>Keep at least 1 copy on hand in case you encounter a Monster House.<br/>Slumber Scroll plays well with a Peach Bun that grants swift speed (ex: Dragon Pit Bun).</p>

<p><b><u>Blank Scroll</u></b><br/>Found on the ground, or by making a Behemoth Gate (22-24F and 31-39F are safer) vanish.<br/>Good eradication targets: Lv2-4 Pop Tanks (40-46F), Skull Lord (66-79F), Archdragon (80-99F).<br/>The first 2 require an incense or Hoppin' Batter family Peach Bun, and the latter has no counter,<br/>since Blast Shield, Shield of Negation, and Dragon Shield can't be found in this dungeon.</p>

<p><b><u>Sanctuary Scroll</u></b><br/>Found on the ground, and can be used on Battler (66-79F) floors to collect bracelets.<br/>However, you'll want to eradicate Skull Lord (66-79F) before eating the Battler Bun.</p>

<h4 id="grass-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/grass.png"/> Grass</h4>

<p><b><u>Invincible Grass</u></b><br/>Nice for situations where you're surrounded, and plays well with Sumo status.<br/>Side note, but Sweet Potato cannot be found in this dungeon.</p>

<p><b><u>Sedating Grass</u></b><br/>Throw it at a monster to disable them for a bit, but don't forget that thrown items can miss.<br/>Can be collected by eating a Sleep Radish Bun (47-50F) and using its ability.</p>

<h4 id="pots-&-incenses-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/pot.png"/> <img src="../images/other/incense.png"/> Pots & Incenses</h4>

<p><b><u>Preservation Pot</u></b><br/>Extremely important, as it lets you protect and carry more Peach Buns, along with other items.<br/>If you encounter a Bouncy Wall Room, strongly consider duplicating Preservation Pots.<br/>It's good to store Mixer Buns so that you can synthesize past 30F if needed.</p>

<p><b><u>Synthesis Pot</u></b><br/>Obtained by making a Behemoth Gate (22-24F and 31-39F are safer) vanish.<br/>Carrying it on Pesky Kappa (24-27F) or Vexing Kappa (55-59F) floors is asking for it to go to waste.</p>

<p><b><u>Evasive Incense, Heat-ban Incense, Reflective Incense</u></b><br/>Evasive Incense is excellent for Sleep Radish (47-50F) floors.<br/>Heat-ban Incense is nice for both Lv2-4 Pop Tank (40-46F) and Archdragon (80-99F) floors.<br/>Reflective Incense counters Skull Lord (66-79F), but you'd need a few to cover the entire range.<br/>Don't forget to rely on specific Peach Buns to counter the above threats too, not just incenses.</p>

<p><b><u>Blurry Incense</u></b><br/>Great for countering single room Monster Houses when Sumo status is active.<br/>Can also be paired with Guiding Staff to quickly and safely reach the stairs.</p>

<h4 id="peach-buns-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/momoman.png"/> Peach Buns</h4>

Eating any non-rotten Peach Bun replenishes 25 fullness and cures all status conditions.<br/>Peach Buns and Onigiri will rot if you enter water after eating an Aquatic type's Peach Bun.

<p><b><u>Rotten Peach Bun</u></b><br/>Obtained by entering water after eating a Spearfish Bun, stepping on a Rotting Trap, etc.<br/>Inflicts 5 damage + a bad status when thrown, which can be used to create and hunt a Cave Mamel.<br/>Possible status conditions include Weakened, Confused, Asleep, Blind, or Deluded status.</p>

<h3 id="travel-companions" class="heading3Pink">Travel Companions</h3>

<p><span class="orangeText">Travel companions only appear in this dungeon on game version 1.1.0 or later.</span><br/>Companions can be used to shield the player character from Scorpion's Strength lowering attack,<br/>Polygon Spinna's Fullness depleting dance, and generally increase player-side damage output.</p>

<h4 id="seki" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/npcs/seki.png"/> Seki</h4>

Seki has Swift action speed and takes 1 step back after using a normal attack against an enemy,<br/>so she safely solos most normal speed monsters that lack a ranged attack, given enough space.<br/>This includes Cave Mamel, powered-up Mixers, foes leveled up via Ghost Samurai, etc.

<h4 id="porky-the-pirate" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/npcs/porky.png"/> Porky the Pirate</h4>

Far lower Max HP than other companions, but throws Porky's Rocks to support the player from afar.<br/>20 damage is significant during early game, but it falls off sharply once tougher monsters show up.<br/>Let Porky the Pirate deal with Ghost Samurai and Behemoth Monsters.

<h4 id="tugai" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/npcs/tugai.png"/> Tugai</h4>

Strong companion who fights with high innate stats and a chance for critical hits.<br/>Throw a Metalhead Bun or swift speed monster's Peach Bun at him to boost his offense.<br/>His ability to take a hit for the player also acts as a bit of insurance.

<h4 id="hibiki" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/npcs/hibiki.png"/> Hibiki</h4>

Reliable companion who supports the player or attacks monsters from a distance.<br/>Her support effects include granting Swift status, inflicting a status, and restoring HP.<br/>Helps with consistency before finding Break-Off Shield or activating Sumo status.

<h3 id="farming-techniques" class="heading3Green">Farming Techniques</h3>

<h4 id="cave-mamel-hunting-(1-2f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/1-3.png"/> Cave Mamel Hunting (1-2F, 7-12F)</h4>

<p><b><u>Rotten Peach Bun Method (1-2F)</u></b><br/>Obtain lots of Peach Buns → eat Spearfish Bun → obtain Rotten Peach Buns by entering water.<br/>Throw Rotten Peach Buns at Pit Mamels (1-3F) until one of them gets Blind status.<br/>Let Pit Mamel slay something to level up → throw Rotten Peach Bun to one-shot Cave Mamel.</p>

<p><b><u>Ghost Samurai Method (7-12F)</u></b><br/>Obtain lots of Pit Mamel Buns on 1-3F → progress to Masked Samurai (7-12F) floors.<br/>Throw a Pit Mamel Bun at a non-Masked Samurai monster → slay a Masked Samurai.<br/>Have the resulting Ghost Samurai possess the Pit Mamel, leveling it up into a Cave Mamel.<br/>Defeat the Cave Mamel for 2,000 experience points.</p>

<h4 id="nigiri-morph-factory-(2-4f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/27-1.png"/> Nigiri Morph Factory (2-4F)</h4>

Nigiri Baby (2-4F) can be used to turn unneeded items into Large Onigiri if you find a shield.<br/>Another option is to eat a Nigiri Baby Bun and transform monsters into Onigiri (1/6 chance).<br/>Increasing Max Fullness to 150+ activates Sumo status, which can be quite helpful.

<h4 id="armordillo-bun-(3-4f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/8-1.png"/> Armordillo Bun (3-4F)</h4>

Use ability for a chance to make a monster trip and drop a random item.<br/>Only 1 item per monster, and tackling also deals 5 damage + 2 tiles of knockback.

<h4 id="karakuroid-bun-removal-trap-(4-6f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/traps/19.png"/> Karakuroid Bun - Removal Trap (4-6F)</h4>

Create Removal Traps → safely check unidentified weapons, shields, and bracelets for curses.

<h4 id="karakuroid-bun-poison-arrows-(4-6f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/traps/3.png"/> Karakuroid Bun - Poison Arrows (4-6F)</h4>

Create Poison Arrow Traps → throw rocks onto traps to collect Poison Arrows.<br/>If you don't have rocks, create traps along a wall, then throw items at the wall.

<h4 id="iron-arrows-(5-7f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/15-2.png"/> Crossbowboy Bun (5-7F)</h4>

Shoot Iron Arrows at a wall → pick up Iron Arrows (aim for 2 or more stacks of arrows).

<h4 id="linger-for-mixers-(5-30f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/45-1.png"/> Linger for Mixers (5-30F)</h4>

This one isn't really a farming technique, but more of a reminder to linger if you want to see Mixers.<br/>Mixer (5-25F, or 5-30F in the extension) is the only floor range where you can synthesize items.<br/>However, it's possible to stock up on Mixer Buns to allow for synthesis between 31-99F too.

<h4 id="porky's-rocks-(8f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/18-1.png"/> Porky Bun (8F, 24-25F), Porko Bun (24-27F)</h4>

Throw Porky's Rocks on the ground → pick up Porky's Rocks (1 stack of rocks is plenty).

<h4 id="hat-prankster-bun-(16-17f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/30-2.png"/> Hat Prankster Bun (16-17F)</h4>

Steal staff or grass category items from monsters (100% success rate, 1 item per monster).

<h4 id="minotaur-hunting-(16-24f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/11-2.png"/> Minotaur Hunting (16-24F)</h4>

Hunt Minotaur (16-24F) in hopes of obtaining an Axe of the Minotaur as a synthesis ingredient.<br/>Axe of the Minotaur is quite rare, though, so don't expect it to drop even if you linger.

<h4 id="battler-bun-(66-79f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/33-3.png"/> Battler Bun (66-79F)</h4>

Can be used to obtain Break-Off Blade / Shield, along with Monster-find and Item-find bracelets.<br/>You'll want to eradicate Skull Lords → place a Sanctuary Scroll on the ground before farming.

# Floor Guide

<h3 id="first-clear" class="heading3Yellow">First Clear</h3>

### General Advice

Many farming techniques can be performed, but most are unnecessary for the 25F first clear.<br/>Special Monster Houses can appear on any floor, so always keep items that counter MHs on hand.<br/>Break-Off Blade & Break-Off Shield + Morphed status can carry you through most floors.

### 1-2F

Enemies aren't tough, so identify items and linger for experience points and Peach Buns.<br/>Pit Mamel Bun (1-3F) can be converted to 2,000 exp each on 7-12F using Masked Samurai.<br/>The best Peach Buns to save are <span class="peachText">Pit Mamel Bun</span> &gt; <span class="peachText">Death Reaper Bun</span> &gt; Others.

If you're feeling adventurous, feel free to attempt the [Cave Mamel Hunting (1-2F)](#cave-mamel-hunting-(1-2f)) strategy.<br/>That said, going to that much effort is not necessary at all for the first clear.

### 4F

Karakuroid (4-6F) doesn't spawn often, but has Swift 1 speed - beware when entering rooms.<br/>If you don't have staves, just throw a weaker monster's Peach Bun (ex: <span class="peachText">Mamel Bun</span>) at Karakuroid.<br/>Karakuroid always spawns with Napping status, and only wakes when Shiren enters or exits the room,<br/>so you can linger without encountering Karakuroids as long as you stay in the same room.

Continue collecting items using <span class="peachText">Armordillo Bun</span> while staying in the stairs room to avoid Karakuroids.<br/>Scorpions (4-7F) can't lower your strength during Morphed status - they inflict Weakened status instead.<br/>In fact, <span class="peachText">Scorpion Bun</span> makes you gain Empowered status whenever you get stung by a Scorpion,<br/>and using its ability lowers an enemy's attack power, serving as a Karakuroid counter.

If you're feeling adventurous, feel free to attempt the [Karakuroid Bun - Poison Arrows (4-6F)](#karakuroid-bun-poison-arrows-(4-6f)) strategy.<br/>That said, going to that much effort is not necessary at all for the first clear.

### 5-6F

The difficulty of the dungeon quickly ramps up starting from this floor range.<br/>Threats on 5-6F include Karakuroid, Bowboy, Crossbowboy, Fresh Octopling, <span class="blueText">Mixer</span>, and Scorpion.

Expect to see Karakuroid (4-6F) with its Swift 1 speed in any new room you step into.<br/>Use <span class="peachText">Scorpion Bun</span> (4-7F) or arrows to deal with them, and stock up on <span class="peachText">Karakuroid Buns</span> if possible.<br/>Collect 1\~2 stacks of Iron Arrows using <span class="peachText">Crossbowboy Bun</span> (5-7F) - shoot arrows at a wall.

Mixer (5-25F) and Fresh Octopling (5-13F) are both very tough when encountered this early.<br/>Take them on using Morphed status if you have a Break-Off Blade and/or Break-Off Shield.<br/>Otherwise, use Karakuroid Bun, Death Reaper Bun, or Pumphantasm Bun to ensure safety.<br/>It might be best to skip synthesis using Mixers until a bit later.

<h3 id="sacred-tree-extension" class="heading3Pink">Sacred Tree Extension</h3>

<h3 id="general-advice-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Red">General Advice</h3>

Overall, this 99F extension is similar to Heart of Serpentcoil Island or Kiki Island,<br/>but with some limitations in exchange for the player being guaranteed a Peach Club.<br/>In particular, Mixer family monsters only appear on 5-30F, making it tricky to upgrade gear.<br/>Chow appears on 15-79F but mostly drops Peach Buns, making it less useful as a source for items.<br/>Strength Grass is also rare, to the point where having 8 Max Strength even late game is common.

All Travel Companions can appear, though Asuka hasn't been sighted earlier than 26F.<br/>These characters boost consistency early game, but can get in the way of farming later on.<br/>The monster table has some notable differences from Heart of Serpentcoil Island:

- Behemoth Gates can appear on 22-24F, 31-39F, 51-54F, 94-98F.
- Behemoth Monster table is similar to Kiki Island's 99F sacred tree extension.
    - B-Sleep Radish, B-Frenzy Radish, and B-Death Reaper are major threats on 51-54F and 94-98F.
- Hoppin' Slugger (30F) only appears on 1 floor, so the Tank Zone (40-46F) is hard to counter.
- Polygon Singa (31-39F) appears for a longer time, but can be countered using Morphed status.
- Lv3 Ninjas spawn on 35-39F instead of 65F (Ninjas no longer appear on 65F).
- Mini Tank (40-44F) appears, making it even harder to protect Travel Companions.
- Sleep Radish (47-50F) appears, but can be countered with Igneous Bun (43-50F).
- The first Lv3 Monk floor range was shortened from 51-59F → 51-55F.
- Thiefwalrus, Greenwalrus, and Ironwalrus no longer appear.
- Lv4 Hat Rascal on 60-65F has been leveled down to Lv3 Hat Brat.
- Lashagga's floor range was changed from 47-54F → 66-79F.

Overall, the idea is to farm useful Peach Buns to supplement and/or make up for a lack of items.<br/>Sumo status is a big help on late game (66-99F) floors, since it's hard to keep up even with Break-Off gear.<br/>There are also fewer initial enemies per floor, and quite a few floor ranges have visible hallways,<br/>including 1-10F, 13F, 30-50F, and 70-99F (So dark hallways = 11-12F, 14-29F, and 51-69F).

<h3 id="60-65f" class="heading3Red">60-65F</h3>

The <span class="pinkText">Item Destruction</span> zone spans from 60-65F, but it has Hat Brat (Lv3) instead of Hat Rascal (Lv4).<br/>There are fewer initial enemies per floor than Heart of Serpentcoil Island, so it's a bit easier here.<br/>However, there's still no reason to linger and farm enemies, so it's best to hurry to the stairs.

Hyper Gazer, Curse Matron, Bag Knave, Hat Brat, and Nigiri Boss all appear between 60-65F.<br/>Hyper Gazer hypnotizes Shiren, forcing him to either attack in a random direction or use an item.<br/>Curse Matron curses up to 2 of your equipped weapon, shield, or bracelets in a single turn.<br/>Bag Knave throws Weeds to knock inventory items away, and turns items on the ground into Weeds.<br/>Hat Brat steals and tosses away a staff, grass, or food category item from your inventory.<br/>Nigiri Boss inflicts Onigiri status instead of transforming inventory items into Large Onigiri.<br/>Onigiri status prevents item usage and nullifies the effects of any equipped items for 10 turns.

<p><span class="peachText">Nigiri Boss Bun</span> makes it so that getting hit by Nigiri Boss's ability grants Empowered status.<br/>Bag Knave can be countered using <span class="peachText">Igneous Bun</span>, <span class="peachText">Hoppin' Slammer Bun</span>, or <span class="peachText">Vexing Kappa Bun</span>.<br/>If you don't have any of the above Peach Buns, just use staves or scrolls when adjacent.<br/><span class="peachText">Hyper Gazer Bun</span> can be used to inflict Confused status on an adjacent enemy.<br/>Curse Matron's ability inflicts Sealed status during Morphed status.</p>

<h3 id="66-79f" class="heading3Red">66-79F</h3>

Power types + Lashagga appear, but unlike other dungeons with a similar table, Behemoths don't spawn.<br/>Lashagga & Miracle Hen (66-79F) deal massive damage during Morphed status with a weak shield,<br/>so be very careful with your decision making if you're running low on items that restore HP.<br/>If you have Poison Arrows or a shield with payback runes, consider staying in base form.<br/>Lv3 Monks appear on 70-75F, and hallways are visible on 70-99F.

Eradication Scroll should be thrown at Skull Lord (66-79F), since there are few ways to block magic.<br/><span class="peachText">Hoppin' Slammer Bun</span> reflects magic, but it's tricky to stay in Morphed status with power types around,<br/>and it's not realistic to stock enough Reflective Incenses to cover the entire Skull Lord floor range.<br/>Miracle Hen hits extremely hard, but it can be significantly weakened with Poison Arrows.<br/>Battler (66-79F) can be dealt with using Morphed status or projectiles from a distance.

If you don't have Poison Arrows, a shield with runes, or useful items, rely on <span class="peachText">Miracle Hen Buns</span>.<br/><span class="peachText">Clapdon Bun</span> (66-79F) can be used to replenish Fullness, or thrown at a monster to instantly weaken it.<br/>However, never eat <span class="peachText">Bellthoven Bun</span> (66-79F) since you'll immediately summon enemies.

<h3 id="80-99f" class="heading3Red">80-99F</h3>

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 94-98F.</span><br/>It's best to act like any nearby Behemoth icons are B-Frenzy Radishes to try and stay safe.<br/>Rush to the stairs using items like Mapping Scroll, <span class="peachText">Dark Owl Bun</span> (25-99F), Narrow-escape Staff, etc.<br/>Wall-less Scroll can be read to make all Behemoth Monsters vanish if you're absolutely cornered.<br/>Hallways are visible on 70-99F, so Behemoth floors hopefully shouldn't be too bad.</p>

Eradication Scroll targets include Archdragon (80-99F) and King Reaper (80-99F).<br/>Archdragon is best, as <span class="peachText">Infernous Bun</span> (80-99F) is tough to obtain without stacking status runes.<br/>King Reaper isn't as hard to deal with thanks to Poison Arrows and Swift status granting Peach Buns.<br/><span class="peachText">Electroid Bun</span> (80-99F) can be used to collect more Poison Arrows if you run out and are desperate.<br/>Not eradicating King Reaper also means you have a chance at obtaining <span class="peachText">King Reaper Buns</span>,<br/>which make exploration trivial with Swift speed, wall-clip, and trap avoidance.

Don't stay in Morphed status if you're not morphed as a monster that grants a meaningful ability.<br/>Instead, use staves or Sumo status to make short work of foes - but beware of Fulminachin (80-99F).<br/>Fulminachin turns red after 100 damage, and explodes when damage received reaches 160+ damage.<br/>If you're struggling to keep up even with Sumo status, focus on Swift Peach Bun evasive play.<br/><span class="peachText">Fluffy Bunny Bun</span> (80-99F)'s healing also might help if you're out of other ideas.
