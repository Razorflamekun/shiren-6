Postgame story dungeon where you play as Kokatsu fused with Shiren (1st clear only).<br/>Kokatsu Shiren can't equip weapons, shields, or bracelets, and also can't use arrows or rocks.<br/>This form isn't simply a restriction, however, as Kokatsu Shiren can use abilities by spending Fullness.<br/>Kokatsu Shiren's normal attack also changes to a flame which increases in range as you slay foes.<br/>Overall, the first clear of this dungeon is a fun diversion from the standard gameplay.

# Strategy

<h3 id="first-clear-vs.-second-clear" class="heading3Yellow">First Clear vs. Second Clear</h3>

As mentioned earlier, <span class="pinkText">the player can't play as Kokatsu Shiren after the first clear in the base game.</span><br/>This is a problem, since the item and monster tables are balanced around Kokatsu Shiren's abilities.<br/>You'll be facing stronger enemies like Nashagga, Mixer, and Hoppin' Slugger without a range advantage,<br/>and most items on the ground are low quality since they expect you to use Sun's Blessing on them.<br/>Equipment is also on the rare side, and it's impossible to create a Cave Mamel to quickly level up.

<h4 id="update-info-(game-version-1.1.0)" class="heading4Yellow">Update Info (Game version 1.1.0)</h4>

[Sacred Tree Mode](/system/sacred-tree) lets you replay the dungeon as Kokatsu Shiren even after the first clear.

## First Clear

<h3 id="kokatsu-shiren-basics" class="heading3Yellow">Kokatsu Shiren Basics</h3>

<div class="characterImage">
  <img src="../images/other/char_kokatsu_shiren.jpg"/>
</div>

<h4 id="innate-attack-&-defense" class="heading4Yellow">Innate Attack Power & Defense Power</h4>

Kokatsu Shiren seems to have a small amount of innate attack and defense compared to Normal Shiren,<br/>which also seems to scale upwards very slowly as Kokatsu Shiren levels up.

<h4 id="item-&-status-restrictions" class="heading4Yellow">Item Category & Status Restrictions</h4>

Kokatsu Shiren can't equip <u>weapon</u>, <u>shield</u>, or <u>bracelet</u> category items, and can't use <u>arrows</u> or <u>rocks</u>.<br/>In addition, Sumo status will not activate even if you increase Max Fullness to 150+.

<h4 id="normal-attack" class="heading4Yellow">Normal Attack Changes</h4>

Kokatsu Shiren's normal attack reaches 1 tile ahead, but gains 1 tile of range per enemy slain (max: 3 tiles).<br/>However, the boosted normal attack range resets to the initial state when Kokatsu Shiren takes damage.<br/>Since Kokatsu Shiren can't equip a shield, it's important to utilize the extended range to avoid damage.<br/>This normal attack can cut through corners, and can also hit Pumphantasms inside walls.

<h4 id="abilities-(special-attacks)" class="heading4Yellow">Abilities (Special Attacks)</h4>

<div class="abilityTable">

|Name|Fullness|Effect|
|-|-|-|
|Sand-handling|10|Creates a pillar of sand in front of you.<br/>If the tile in front is a wall, digs out the wall tile.|
|Deception|20|Creates a decoy (50 HP).<br/>Decoy receives normal amount of damage, unlike Disguising Staff.<br/>Decoy loses 2 HP per turn, and a monster that slays it won't level up.|

<h3 class="heading3Purple">Important Items (Kokatsu Shiren)</h3>

The special attack "Sun's Blessing" is the key to stocking up on most of the following items.<br/>However, it often gives mediocre items as well, so don't expect only useful items when you use it.

<h4 id="grass" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/grass.png"/> Grass</h4>

<p><b><u>Revival Grass, Invincible Grass, Swift Grass, Leaping Grass, Power-up Grass</u></b><br/>These are all consumable items that help you out in tricky situations one way or another.<br/>Invincible, Swift, Power-up, and Revival grasses are also great for the boss fight.</p>

<p><b><u>Life Grass, Healing Grass, Otogirisou, Herb, Strength Grass, Fortune Grass</u></b><br/>Without access to shields, increasing Max HP is the main way to improve Shiren's survivability.<br/>Likewise, increasing Shiren's level and strength is the only way to deal more damage.</p>

<h4 id="food" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/food.png"/> Food</h4>

<p><b><u>Onigiri, Large Onigiri, Grilled Onigiri, Special Onigiri</u></b><br/>Since Fullness is consumed to fuel special attacks, aim to keep 1~3 onigiri on hand at all times.<br/>Onigiri can be grilled using an Explochin (9-15F) or Explosion Trap if desired, but it can be a bit risky.<br/>Special Onigiri's Knowledgeable status bonus is helpful early on, but it only replenishes 30 Fullness,<br/>so prioritize more filling onigiri items as you descend further into the dungeon.</p>

<h4 id="staff" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> Staves</h4>

<p><b><u>Peach Staff</u></b><br/>If you're lucky enough to find this staff, you can perform additional <a href="#farming-techniques-(kokatsu-shiren)">Farming Techniques</a>.<br/>Of course, being able to one-shot any monster besides Hoppin' Batter types is handy as well.</p>

<h4 id="scrolls" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> Scrolls</h4>

<p><b><u>Mapping Scroll</u></b><br/>Being able to see where enemies are located makes it easier to maintain 3 tile normal attacks.<br/>Most monsters in this dungeon don't have ranged attacks, so it puts you at a big advantage.<br/>It's recommended to linger and hunt monsters whenever you use one of these scrolls.</p>

<p><b><u>Other Scrolls</u></b><br/>Windblade Scroll isn't essential since you have access to Blazing Heat, but it does conserve Fullness.<br/>Blank Scroll should probably be used as an Eradication Scroll if you have it unlocked.<br/>Equipment related scrolls are simply fodder for Sun's Blessing.</p>

<h4 id="pots-&-gitan" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/pot.png"/> <img src="../images/other/gitan.png"/> Pots & Gitan</h4>

<p><b><u>Cashing Pot, Gitan</u></b><br/>Kokatsu Shiren can throw Gitan bags just like Normal Shiren.<br/>If you find 2 Fortune Grass, throw them at Super Gazer (16-20F) and hunt the Ultra Gazer for 3,000 Gitan.<br/>Froggo family monsters appear throughout the dungeon, so it's not too hard to come by Gitan bags either.<br/>Insert weapons, shields, bracelets, and Special Onigiri into Cashing Pots to create high value Gitan bags,<br/>then throw said bags during the boss fight to make quick work of Embodiment of Suiryu.</p>

<h3 id="farming-techniques-(kokatsu-shiren)" class="heading3Green">Farming Techniques (Kokatsu Shiren)</h3>

<h4 id="poison-grass-(6-11f)-/-confusion-grass-(16-19f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/20-1.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/20-2.png"/> Poison Grass (6-11F) / Confusion Grass (16-19F)</h4>

If you find a Peach Staff, turn into a Ghost Radish (6-11F) or Daze Radish (16-19F) and throw grass,<br/>then use Sun's Blessing on the Poison Grass or Confusion Grass to obtain better grass and food items.<br/>Chow, Gazer, Mudkin, or Explochin can cause issues while you do this, so be sure to seal off the room<br/>using Sand-handling beforehand - and check for hidden traps if you want to be extra safe.<br/>Don't forget that Seewell Grass's effect wears off when you eat a Peach Bun.

<h4 id="mudster-/-mudder-hunting-(6-11f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/32-3.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/32-4.png"/> Mudster / Mudder Hunting (6-11F)</h4>

Mudkin (6-11F)'s special attack lowers Kokatsu Shiren's attack and defense.<br/>So the idea is to intentionally get weakened by Mudkin a few times, then attack to make them multiply.<br/>Once there are lots of Lv1 Mudkin, use Blazing Heat once to blind them and create a Lv2 Mudkin,<br/>then repeat the process until you ultimately have a bunch of Lv3 or Lv4 Mudkin in the room.<br/>At that point, wipe out all remaining monsters using Blazing Heat to earn lots of experience points.<br/>Seal off the room using Sand-handling beforehand, and stand on the stairs in case it gets out of control.<br/>Lv3-4 Mudkin require 3 uses of Blazing Heat, so you'll likely need at least 300\~350 Fullness.

<h4 id="item-drop-hunting-(6-19f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/28-1.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/28-2.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/48-1.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/29-1.png"/> Item Drop Hunting (6-19F)</h4>

Hunt Froggo (6-10F), Chow (6-10F, 16-19F), Thiefwalrus (9-19F), and Froggucchi (16-19F) for extra items.<br/>Not much to say about this one, other than try to maintain 3 tile range to avoid their stealing attacks.

<h4 id="3,000-gitan-bag-(16-19f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/22-4.png"/> 3,000 Gitan Bag (16-19F)</h4>

Throw 2 Fortune Grass obtained via Sun's Blessing at a Super Gazer (16-20F) to create an Ultra Gazer,<br/>then simply defeat the Ultra Gazer using Blazing Heat or such to secure a Gitan bag worth 3,000 Gitan.<br/>Embodiment of Suiryu only has 555 HP, while 3,000 Gitan deals 600 damage - enough to one-shot Suiryu.<br/>It's best to hunt 2 Ultra Gazers so you can throw 2 bags in a row, in case the first one misses.

<h4 id="sand-handling-&-arrow-traps" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/traps/2.png"/> <img src="../images/traps/3.png"/> Sand-handling & Arrow Traps</h4>

Use Seewell Grass to find an arrow trap, then create a pillar next to it and throw items at the pillar,<br/>followed by performing Sun's Blessing on each of the single arrows that land on the ground.

<h4 id="monstercall-scroll-&-blazing-heat" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> Monstercall Scroll & Blazing Heat</h4>

Write Monstercall Scroll on a Blank Scroll, then use Blazing Heat to clear out the Monster House.<br/>Generally more practical during early game, since it takes more uses of Blazing Heat later on.

## Second Clear

<h3 id="important-items-(normal-shiren)" class="heading3Pink">Important Items (Normal Shiren)</h3>

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/weapon/13.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/17.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>Cyclops Bane, Gazer Shield</u></b><br/>Either item is a significant help against Gazer (4-8F) and Super Gazer (16-20F).</p>

<p><b><u>Watersplitter</u></b><br/>Aquatic type monsters appear on all floors, and even the boss is an Aquatic type.<br/>You can activate the damage boost resonance effect if a Spearfish drops a Spearscale Shield.</p>

<p><b><u>Sickle of Salvation</u></b><br/>Nice for Pumphantasm (4-8F), Ghost Radish (6-11F), Pumpanshee (12-19F), and Daze Radish (16-20F).<br/>Masked Samurai doesn't appear in this dungeon, so don't worry about handling Ghost Samurai.</p>

<h4 id="food-(normal-shiren)" class="heading4Pink"><img src="../images/other/food.png"/> Food</h4>

<p><b><u>Special Onigiri</u></b><br/>Satiated status is valuable, since food is on the uncommon side here.<br/>It's best to eat it at the start of a floor if you want to try and maximize potential benefits.</p>

<h3 id="farming-techniques-(normal-shiren)" class="heading3Red">Farming Techniques (Normal Shiren)</h3>

<h4 id="mudster-hunting-(6-11f)" class="heading4Red"><img src="../images/monsters/32-3.png"/> Mudster Hunting (6-11F)</h4>

You can level up a Mudkin (6-11F) by inflicting Blind status (Blinding Grass, Rotten Onigiri, etc.),<br/>inflicting Confused status (Confusion Grass, Confusion Scroll, etc.), or by throwing Fortune Grass.<br/>However, it's rare to find multiples of such items, so you're unlikely to be able to do this in practice.<br/>If you're determined, search for a Rotting Trap and make it a point to collect lots of Rotten Onigiri.<br/>As always, unequip your gear and stand on the stairs when punching Mudsters for exp.

<h4 id="item-drop-hunting-(6-19f)-(normal-shiren)" class="heading4Red"><img src="../images/monsters/28-1.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/28-2.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/48-1.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/29-1.png"/> Item Drop Hunting (6-19F)</h4>

Hunt Froggo (6-10F), Chow (6-10F, 16-19F), Thiefwalrus (9-19F), and Froggucchi (16-19F) for extra items.<br/>You won't have a range advantage as Normal Shiren unless you lucked out and found arrows or rocks,<br/>so consider throwing Gitan bags from Froggo family monsters at Thiefwalrus to ensure item drops.

<h4 id="3,000-gitan-bag-(16-19f)-(normal-shiren)" class="heading4Red"><img src="../images/monsters/22-4.png"/> 3,000 Gitan Bag (16-19F)</h4>

Throw Rotten Onigiri at Super Gazer (16-20F) to inflict Blind status and have it level up into an Ultra Gazer,<br/>then use items like Sealing Staff and Sluggish Staff to slay the Ultra Gazer for a 3,000 Gitan bag drop.<br/>With lots of luck, a Super Gazer might randomly defeat a Chow (16-19F) and level up on its own instead.<br/>Embodiment of Suiryu only has 555 HP, while 3,000 Gitan deals 600 damage - enough to one-shot Suiryu.

<h4 id="rocks-&-arrow-traps" class="heading4Red"><img src="../images/other/rock.png"/> <img src="../images/traps/2.png"/> <img src="../images/traps/3.png"/> Rocks & Arrow Traps</h4>

Use Seewell Grass to find an arrow trap, then throw rocks onto the trap to collect lots of arrows.<br/>Alternatively, run column by column or row by row in a room to hopefully step on a hidden arrow trap.<br/>However, the second method isn't recommended because food isn't super common in this dungeon,<br/>and you also risk stepping on a negative trap like Rotting Trap, Curse Trap, or Onigiri Trap.

<h4 id="rotten-onigiri" class="heading4Red"><img src="../images/traps/21.png"/> Rotten Onigiri</h4>

Use Seewell Grass to find a Rotting Trap, then lure a monster onto the trap and use projectiles.<br/>If the projectile misses, it'll land on the trap instead, triggering it and creating a Rotten Onigiri.<br/>It's quite risky, but Explochin (9-15F) can be used to grill Rotten Onigiri if desired.

# Floor Guide

### 1-5F

Remember that Kokatsu Shiren's normal attack can cut corners and hit enemies that are in walls.<br/>It's fine to use staves and grass items to identify them - at worst, you might eat a bad status grass.<br/>Alternatively, collect unidentified items and then try for Special Onigiri's Knowledgeable status bonus.<br/>Use equipment and Gitan as fodder for Sun's Blessing - dash to step on Gitan without picking it up.<br/>If you find a shop, sell Special Onigiri obtained from Sun's Blessing to secure funds for shopping.

Kokatsu Shiren levels up at a much faster pace than Normal Shiren up until Lv25.<br/>Pumphantasm (4-8F) and Gazer (4-8F) are both tougher, so aim to reach Lv7 by the end of 3F.<br/>Hoppin' Hitter (5-8F) is even stronger, so aim to reach Lv10 for faster HP regen before 5F.

### 6-11F

This is usually the last floor range where you can comfortably keep up with enemies,<br/>so use this floor range to finalize stocking up on food and grass items for the second half.<br/>If you have a Peach Staff, morph into Ghost Radish (6-11F) and throw Poison Grass for Sun's Blessing.<br/>Otherwise, check rooms for items while being careful about groups of enemies.

Hunt Mudkins (6-19F) using the method explained in an earlier section to level up if possible.<br/>Chow (6-10F) can cause a different monster to level up, so keep a close eye on the message log.<br/>Remember that you can use Deception or Blazing Heat to deal with a leveled up monster if needed.

Nashagga (9-15F) attacks 2 times per turn. It hurts - don't be deceived by its lower attack stat.<br/>Explochin (9-15F) turns red when HP &le; 50%, and explodes when HP &le; 24 (96+ damage dealt).<br/>The explosion can be used to grill onigiri if you stepped on a Rotting Trap, but your HP will be set to 1.

### 12-15F

Chow exits the monster table in this range, so you don't need to worry about monsters leveling up.<br/>Mixer (12-19F) and Pumpanshee (12-19F) have higher attack power than Nashagga and Explochin.<br/>Don't just trade hits with them - fight with a range advantage, or use an ability or item.

If you have food and see an opportunity to seal off the stairs room to hunt Mudkins, go ahead and do so,<br/>but remember to keep onigiri on hand for the boss fight too.

### 16-19F

Mixer and Pumpanshee are still around, and Chow (16-19F) returns.<br/>Rush to the stairs using any Mapping Scrolls and Seewell Grass you saved for this floor range.<br/>Don't hesitate to use an ability or item before things becomes dire if you end up in a bad situation.<br/>Also, boost your normal attack range and replenish Fullness before descending the stairs on 19F.

If you're feeling adventurous, throw 2 Fortune Grass at a Super Gazer to create an Ultra Gazer,<br/>then hunt the Ultra Gazer to secure a bag of 3,000 Gitan → throw the bag at the boss to one-shot it.<br/>You can safely hunt the Ultra Gazer by using Blazing Heat (inflicts Blind status) or such.

Hoppin' Slugger (16-19F) nullifies or reflects magic bullets and thrown items.<br/>Capt. Spearfish (16-20F) has high survivability thanks to its underwater HP regen.<br/>Daze Radish (16-20F) throws Confusion Grass → Poison Grass within a 2 tile radius.<br/>Dartingfrog (18-20F) pulls you closer and attacks from 5 tiles ahead, negating any range advantage.<br/>Super Gazer (16-20F) hypnotizes you and makes you either attack a random direction or use an item.

### Boss (20F)

<div class="dungeonPageImage">
  <img src="../images/areas/other/suiryu_boss.jpg"/>
</div>

Embodiment of Suiryu (555 HP, 40 atk, 22 def) is an Aquatic and Dragon dual-type monster.<br/>Status conditions can be inflicted, but they get cured in roughly 2\~4 turns depending on the status.<br/>Sanctuary Scroll and Knockback Staff <span class="redText">do not</span> work, but Paralyzing Staff and Narrow-escape Staff <span class="blueText">do</span> work.

Special attacks:

- [Content]

Other notes:

- Suiryu's bad status conditions will automatically be cured if she's on a water tile.
- Blazing Heat can be used to blind all regular monsters on the field at the cost of 60 Fullness.
    - Blind status doesn't wear off as turns elapse, so it's great for shutting down special attacks.
- The boss shuffles character positions and soaks your inventory after a few turns.
    - So if you plan on using scrolls, it's best to read them near the start of the fight.
- Swift Grass, Power-up Grass, and Invincible Grass are fantastic.
    - Aggressive Incense can be paired with Invincible Grass to quickly deal massive damage.
- Empathetic Staff can be used to supplement damage if your damage output is low.
- Thrown Gitan is effective against the boss.
    - If you want to try this strategy, you can hunt Froggucchis on 16-19F.
