Postgame dungeon where the goal is to identify 50 items, and the reward is the last item identified.<br/>This dungeon utilizes an inference mechanic which narrows down unidentified items to 3 possibilities.<br/>The last item identified by the player doesn't have to be inside the inventory to be received as a reward.<br/>Most runs of this dungeon can be cleared around 9\~12F if you identify items as you pick them up.<br/>Overall, it's a great place to visit to fill out some of the rarer item entries in the notebook.

# Strategy

<h3 id="dungeon-characteristics" class="heading3Yellow">Dungeon Characteristics</h3>

<h4 id="changes-from-training-path-of-inference" class="heading4Yellow">Changes from Training Path of Inference</h4>

The rules are similar to [Training Path of Inference](/dungeons/training-path-of-inference), but with the following differences:

- The dungeon is cleared as soon as the player has identified 50 items (average: \~10F).
    - It's also possible to clear the dungeon by descending the stairs on 20F.
    - The message "You have N items left to identify!" is displayed after identifying an item.
- The last item identified becomes the reward, and the quantity depends on the clear depth.
    - If the last item was a pot, the contents of the pot are not included in the reward.
    - Fully ID'ing your inventory with a lucky roll of Identifier Scroll still only rewards 1 type of item.
    - Identifying a disguised N'dulu as the last item results in receiving a real item, not a N'dulu.
    - Weapons, shields, projectiles, food, and Blank Scrolls cannot be obtained as a reward.
    - It's been said that descending the stairs without ID'ing any items rewards you with<br/>the last item identified during a previous adventure.
- One random unidentified item gets auto-identified when the player advances to the next floor.
    - Items in pots are excluded, so if you want to save a specific item for last, keep it in a pot.
        - <span class="blueText">Safe</span> = Preservation, Ordinary, Exorcism, Synthesis, Upgrading, Degrading, or Walrus.
        - <span class="redText">Unsafe</span> = Identifier, Transmutation, Cashing, Unbreakable, or Curse (if ID'ing via usage).
    - <span class="pinkText">An item will not get auto-identified when you descend stairs on a final floor.</span>
    <ul>
      <li>Final floors = 20F (First Clear), or 50F (Sacred Tree Extension).</li>
      <li>Identifier Scroll can be used to take home a bracelet, staff, or pot found on a final floor.</li>
      <li>For other categories, either use the item or insert the item into an Identifier Pot.</li>
    </ul>
- Writing Escape Scroll on a Blank Scroll and reading it results in the effect being blocked.
- Hallways are dark on all floors.
    - There's greater risk of being hit by a surprise attack from Gazer, Nigiri Morph, etc.
- The item table features more items, including rarer finds like Appraiser's Bracelet.
    - Appraiser's Bracelet in particular can only be found from 13F onwards.
    - In Sacred Tree Mode, Appraiser's Bracelet has a very high chance of being found on 50F.
        - It's assumed to be a 100% chance, but it hasn't actually been datamined as of now.
    - This was the premier spot where Appraiser's Bracelet could be found in the base game,<br/>but this bracelet can now be found in other dungeons that were added via updates.
- The monster table has been remixed, though it's still pretty similar overall.
    - N'dubba (8-11F) is removed, N'dulu (17-20F) is added.
    - Dragon (15-20F) appears earlier in the first clear, instead of only appearing on 20F.
    - Super Gazer, Nigiri Boss, and Pesky Kappa no longer appear on 20F.
    - Nashagga (7-14F), Brodillo (10-14F), and other foes have adjusted spawn floors.
        - In the original dungeon, Nashagga was 7-13F, and Brodillo was 10-20F.
    - The monster table from 21F onwards in [Sacred Tree Mode](/system/sacred-tree) also differs.
- Rescue requests are not allowed, Roamer NPCs don't appear, and the floor BGM is different.
- This dungeon cannot be selected in Monster Trial Mode.

<h4 id="inference-mechanic" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/weapon/unknown.png"/> Inference Mechanic</h4>

New gameplay mechanic introduced in Shiren 6 that is enabled only in certain dungeons.<br/>Check an unidentified item's description to view 3 possibilities as to what the item could be,<br/>then choose how to best use the item based on the list of possibilities.

Example:

|Item Name|
|-|
|Herb|
|Dragon Grass|
|Sedating Grass|

So in this case, you wouldn't want to risk using it when an enemy is adjacent due to Sedating Grass.<br/>Both Sedating Grass and Dragon Grass are good when thrown, and throwing an Herb is harmless<br/>if the target is already at full HP, so you might want to lean towards throwing this grass item.

Unfortunately, item names assigned by the player are not reflected in the list of possibilities.<br/>If a possibility has already been fully identified, the possibility will be greyed-out.

<h4 id="note-about-item-variety" class="heading4Yellow">Note About Item Variety</h4>

Clairvoyant Bracelet, Sure-aim Bracelet, Appraiser's Bracelet, Upgrading Pot, Monstercall Scroll,<br/>and other uncommon or outright rare items appear - the item table rivals [Heart of Serpentcoil Island](/dungeons/heart-of-serpentcoil-island).<br/>Most bracelets, scrolls, and pots can be found, so it's a great place for filling out notebook entries.<br/>All said, the only item categories that notably lack in variety here are weapons and shields.

<h3 id="strategy-#1-quick-clear" class="heading3Yellow">Strategy #1 - Quick Clear</h3>

If you're only interested in clearing the dungeon as fast as possible, focus on identifying 50 items.<br/>You'll usually end up clearing the dungeon at around half the depth if you identify all possible items.<br/>Create safe situations to test unidentified items in instead of focusing on conserving items.

- Grass, Scroll, Incense, Pot (<span class="blueText">Tap</span> Type) → Use before advancing to the next floor.
    - If an item is cursed, insert it into a pot (<span class="blueText">Put In</span> Type) or light an incense with it.
- Identifier Scroll → Use on Pots (<span class="blueText">Put In</span> Type).
- Identifier Pot → Insert bracelets or staves.

The item that gets auto-ID'd when changing floors will be selected from whatever is left after said tests.

<h3 id="strategy-#2-specific-reward" class="heading3Yellow">Strategy #2 - Specific Reward</h3>

Taking home a specific reward requires more strategy compared to simply clearing the dungeon,<br/>as the player needs to ensure the item they're aiming for doesn't get identified until the very end.<br/><span class="orangeText">This is the strategy you want to use for taking home an Appraiser's Bracelet (13-50F).</span>

<h4 id="basic-strategy-(not-appraiser's-bracelet-on-50f)" class="heading4Yellow">Basic Strategy (Not Appraiser's Bracelet on 50F)</h4>

1. Identify 49 items, obtain the target item.
    - Keep the target item in a non-item-altering pot so that it doesn't get auto-identified.
        - <span class="blueText">Safe</span> = Preservation, Ordinary, Exorcism, Synthesis, Upgrading, Degrading, or Walrus.
        - <span class="redText">Unsafe</span> = Identifier, Transmutation, Cashing, Unbreakable, or Curse (if ID'ing via usage).
    - Always account for the possibility of a lucky use when reading an Identifier Scroll.
        - If the Identifier Scroll is blessed, it has a 100% chance for a lucky use.
    - If the target item is a pot or incense, keep at least 1 other unidentified item on hand.
        - You'll have to hope the target item doesn't get auto-identified when changing floors.
2. Place all unidentified items down except for the target item.
3. Choose one of the following steps:
    - Advance floors → Target item will be identified automatically, clearing the dungeon.
        - <span class="pinkText">However, auto-ID does not occur when you use the stairs on a final floor.</span>
        - Final floors = 20F (First Clear), or 50F (Sacred Tree Extension).
    - Read an Identifier Scroll → Target item will be the only item identified even with a lucky use.
    - Insert into an Identifier Pot → Target item will be the only item identified.

<h4 id="appraiser's-bracelet-(found-on-50f)" class="heading4Yellow">Appraiser's Bracelet (Found on 50F)</h4>

1. Identify 49 items, find a Collection Scroll (or Evasive Incense) + Identifier Scroll or Identifier Pot.
    - <span class="pinkText">Collection Scroll (or Evasive Incense) is needed to counter Vexing Kappa (40-50F).</span>
3. Read the Collection Scroll at the start of 50F, then pick up the Appraiser's Bracelet.
    - The Collection Scroll must already be ID'd, or else it'll increase the ID'd items count.
4. Place all unidentified items down except for the Appraiser's Bracelet.
5. Choose one of the following steps (<span class="redText">Do not use the stairs on 50F</span>):
    - Read an Identifier Scroll → The bracelet will be the only item identified even with a lucky use.
    - Insert into an Identifier Pot → The bracelet will be the only item identified.

<h4 id="recovering-from-accidental-early-target-identification" class="heading4Yellow">Recovering from Accidental Early Target Identification</h4>

If the target item became identified early, don't give up! - It's fine as long as nothing else gets identified.<br/>This strategy can be used for any item category, but can be difficult depending on ID progress.

1. Place all unidentified items down, and don't pick up any new unidentified items.
    - This includes any items you assigned names to that have <span class="greenText">green names</span>.
2. Rush to the stairs on all floors until you descend past the final floor, clearing the dungeon.

<h4 id="parallel-play-for-appraiser's-bracelet" class="heading4Yellow">Parallel Play for Appraiser's Bracelet</h4>

The idea is to prepare a Monstercall Scroll and use Parallel Play to RNG manipulate for it.

Preparation:

- <u>Monstercall Scroll</u>
- <u>Identifier Scroll</u> or <u>Identifier Pot</u>
- Items that let you successfully tackle a Monster House.
    - Blank Scroll (write Expulsion Scroll) + Seewell Grass is the ideal combo.
- 49 items identified so you can quickly clear the dungeon.

Steps:

1. Create a Parallel Play data on a floor in the range of 13-50F (No need to upload).
2. Suspend your real adventure, and resume from the Parallel Play data.
3. Read the Monstercall Scroll.
4. If an Appraiser's Bracelet <span class="redText">was not</span> generated:
    1. Give up, then resume from the same Parallel Play data again.
    2. Perform an action (example: normal attack) to advance the turn RNG.
        - The action you choose here must be repeated as a chain during each subsequent attempt.
    3. Repeat from step 3.
5. If an Appraiser's Bracelet <span class="blueText">was</span> generated:
    1. Resume your real adventure.
    2. Perform the <b><u>exact same actions</u></b> you did during the previous Parallel Play adventure.
6. Carefully identify the Appraiser's Bracelet to take it home as a reward.

Example:

- Prep (Real) = Monstercall Scroll in inventory on 13F → create Parallel Data → suspend.
- Attempt 1 (Parallel) = read Monstercall Scroll → <span class="highlightPink">no bracelet...</span>
- Attempt 2 (Parallel) = normal attack x <span class="goldText">1</span> → read Monstercall Scroll → <span class="highlightPink">no bracelet...</span>
- Attempt 3 (Parallel) = normal attack x <span class="orangeText">2</span> → read Monstercall Scroll → <span class="highlightPink">no bracelet...</span>
- Attempt 4 (Parallel) = normal attack x <span class="redText">3</span> → read Monstercall Scroll → <span class="highlightGreen">bracelet found!</span>
- Attempt 4b (Real) = normal attack x <span class="redText">3</span> → read Monstercall Scroll → <span class="highlightGreen">bracelet found!</span>

<h3 id="important-items-(first-clear)" class="heading3Purple">Important Items (First Clear)</h3>

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/weapon/2.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/1.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>High Base Value Items</u></b><br/>Bronze Sword and Wooden Shield are often found between 1-5F, but everything else is rare,<br/>making it rather difficult to activate a resonance effect that increases attack power and defense.<br/>If you don't find the 2nd item in a resonance set, just use the highest base value items you find.</p>

<p><b><u>Watchful Shield, Counter Shield</u></b><br/>Low base value, but the innate rune on either shield gets immediate results.<br/>It's safe to say that these should be prioritized before Wooden Shield, at least.</p>

<p><b><u>Gazer Shield</u></b><br/>Equip this shield before becoming adjacent to Gazer (8-20F) to avoid its hypnosis.<br/>It goes without saying, but swap to your strongest shield before fighting other monsters.</p>

<p><b><u>Dragonkiller, Dragon Shield</u></b><br/>Solid base value, great against Nashagga (7-14F) and Dragon (15-20F).</p>

<p><b><u>Shield of Sating</u></b><br/>Makes it easier to maintain Sumo status, which is great for mid~late game floors.<br/>Low base value, so it's best to swap to a stronger shield before fighting.</p>

<h4 id="bracelets-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/bracelet.png"/> Bracelets</h4>

<p><b><u>Monster-find Bracelet, Item-find Bracelet, Clairvoyant Bracelet</u></b><br/>Monster-find Bracelet lets you avoid encounters, allowing for more time to regenerate HP.<br/>Item-find Bracelet is useful for planning a more efficient route at the start of a new floor.</p>

<p><b><u>Wallpass Bracelet</u></b><br/>This bracelet lets you have a one-sided fight against most monsters if there's a wall tile.</p>

<p><b><u>Appraiser's Bracelet</u></b><br/><span class="pinkText">Only found from 13F onwards</span> - It's not guaranteed to appear during a run of the first clear.<br/>Use an Identifier Scroll or Identifier Pot just before clearing the dungeon to take it home.</p>

<h4 id="scrolls-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> Scrolls</h4>

<p><b><u>Identifier Scroll</u></b><br/>It's best to identify this early on if you want to take home a specific reward.<br/>If the scroll is blessed and you select an unidentified item, all inventory items will get identified.</p>

<p><b><u>Eradication Scroll</u></b><br/>Popular targets that reduce the risk of item loss include Gazer (8-20F) and Nigiri Morph (15-20F).<br/>Otherwise, some players eradicate Nashagga (7-14F) or Dragon (15-20F) if they lack a Dragon Shield.</p>

<p><b><u>Blank Scroll</u></b><br/>Always found in an identified state, so you can't take it home as a reward.<br/>Select <span class="blueText">Write</span> to instantly check whether or not the scroll is blessed or cursed.<br/>If it's blessed, consider writing Identifier Scroll to identify your entire inventory at once.<br/>If you need to register Eradication Scroll, do a few runs of Domain of Staves and Scrolls beforehand.<br/>It might be best to write and use it before Gazer (8-20F) forces you to read it while hypnotized.</p>

<h4 id="staves-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> Staves</h4>

<p><b><u>Paralyzing Staff, Narrow-escape Staff, Unlucky Staff, Sealing Staff, Sluggish Staff</u></b><br/>Staves that excel at disabling a single target - especially nice against stronger monsters near the end.<br/>Unlucky Staff targets: Nashagga (7-14F), Brodillo (10-14F), Polygon Shaka (15-20F), Nigiri Morph (15-20F).<br/>Keep in mind that slowed monsters can be used to stall out pursuing enemies in a hallway, too.</p>

<h4 id="pots-&-incenses-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/pot.png"/> Pots & Incenses</h4>

<p><b><u>Identifier Pot</u></b><br/>Useful when an unidentified item's possibilities is on the tricky side to narrow down without help.<br/>Can also be used to check for curses on very important items like Revival Grass.</p>

<p><b><u>Preservation Pot</u></b><br/>Best pot to use as a container to prevent auto-identification from targeting specific items.<br/>Other pots that act as storage include Ordinary, Exorcism, Synthesis, Upgrading, Degrading, and Walrus.</p>

<p><b><u>Water Gun Pot</u></b><br/>Can be used to significantly lower Nashagga (7-14F)'s attack power or one-shot Fierous (10-16F).<br/>Deals 5 damage and has 100% accuracy, so it can also be used to instantly slay a Cave Mamel.</p>

<p><b><u>Blurry Incense</u></b><br/>Pair it with Monster-find Bracelet or Clairvoyant Bracelet to safely ignore monsters in a room.<br/>Also plays well with Guiding Staff, if you're lucky enough to find both items.</p>

<h4 id="farming-related-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/grass.png"/> <img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> <img src="../images/other/incense.png"/> Farming Related</h4>

<p><b><u>Fortune Grass, Angel Seed, Disguising Staff, Blinding Grass, Berserk Seed, Jitters Scroll</u></b><br/>Some of the items that can be utilized to level up a Mamel (1-3F) or Pit Mamel (1-3F) into a Cave Mamel.<br/>Other options include Confusion Grass, Confusion Scroll, and Rotten Onigiri, but they're less reliable.</p>

<p><b><u>Sluggish Staff, Knockback Staff, Burrowing Staff, Thunderbolt Staff, Rock, Rotten Onigiri, Gitan</u></b><br/>Some of the items that can be used to safely defeat a Cave Mamel for 2,000 experience points.<br/>Remember that Rocks, Rotten Onigiri, and Gitan can miss - don't risk it when adjacent.<br/>Another 100% accuracy option aside from staves is Water Gun Pot.</p>

<p><b><u>Fortune Staff</u></b><br/>Can be used to level up a Pit Mamel (1-3F) into a Cave Mamel for experience points.<br/>It's worth keeping Fortune Staff on hand even if you've gone past the floors where Pit Mamel appears,<br/>since it can be paired with Reflective Incense to hit yourself with magic bullets to rapidly level up.</p>

<p><b><u>Reflective Incense</u></b><br/>Light the incense, then swing a Fortune Staff at a foe to hit yourself with magic bullets and level up.<br/>Reflects Gazer (8-20F), Curse Girl (13-20F), and Polygon Shaka (15-20F)'s special attacks.<br/>Don't forget that player-side magic bullets from swinging staves will also be reflected.</p>

<p><b><u>Cautious Incense</u></b><br/>Stops Thiefwalrus (8-11F) or Greenwalrus (16-20F) from being able to steal inventory items.<br/>That said, you likely won't have the means to linger and farm Thiefwalrus family monsters,<br/>regardless of whether or not you have a Cautious Incense on hand.</p>

<p><b><u>Monstercall Scroll, Walrus Pot, Transmutation Pot</u></b><br/>Some of the items that can be used to generate additional items during a run.<br/>If you decide to read a Monstercall Scroll, follow it up with items that quickly wipe out the room.<br/>Walrus Pot should be thrown against a wall → defeat the Thiefwalrus monsters for item drops.</p>

<h3 id="important-items-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Blue">Important Items (Sacred Tree Extension)</h3>

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/weapon/15.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/10.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>Dragonkiller, Dragon Shield</u></b><br/>Solid base value, great against Nashagga (7-14F), Dragon (15-39F), and Sky Dragon (40-50F).<br/>Arguably the best main weapon for this dungeon, especially if you activate the resonance effect.</p>

<p><b><u>Watchful Shield, Counter Shield</u></b><br/>These shields have special abilities that provide an advantage during combat even with low defense,<br/>or when Pesky Kappa (21-39F), Vexing Kappa (40-50F), or Sleep Radish (40-50F) inflicts a status.</p>

<p><b><u>Cyclops Bane, Gazer Shield</u></b><br/>Secondary gear that should be used against Gazer (8-20F) and Super Gazer (21-39F).<br/>Cyclops Bane is also effective against Metalhead (10-18F).</p>

<p><b><u>Sickle of Salvation</u></b><br/>Shines against Soul Reaper (40-50F) and Sleep Radish (40-50F) near the end.<br/>Probably best wielded as a rune instead of using it as a main weapon.</p>

<p><b><u>Shield of Sating</u></b><br/>Makes it easier to maintain Sumo status, which is great for mid~late game floors.<br/>However, Sumo status isn't a free win thanks to dark hallways + Nigiri Boss's Onigiri status.<br/>Probably best wielded as a rune instead of using it as a main shield.</p>

<p><b><u>Kappa's Dish</u></b><br/>Boosts your chance of dodging projectiles aimed at you, which is great on 21-50F.<br/>Applicable to Pesky Kappa (21-39F), Vexing Kappa (40-50F), and Sleep Radish (40-50F).<br/>That said, dodging projectiles isn't guaranteed, so think of it as chance-based insurance.</p>

<h4 id="bracelets-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/bracelet.png"/> Bracelets</h4>

<p><b><u>Wallpass Bracelet</u></b><br/>Fantastic bracelet that lets you have a one-sided fight against most monsters if there's a wall tile.<br/>Exceptions: Pesky Kappa (21-39F), Vexing Kappa (40-50F), Sleep Radish (40-50F), Soul Reaper (40-50F).</p>

<p><b><u>Clairvoyant Bracelet</u></b><br/>Offers enhanced exploration efficiency, letting you avoid enemies or plan better routes for items.<br/>Also clues you in on incoming Sleep Radish (40-50F) and Soul Reaper (40-50F) combinations,<br/>which not even Wallpass Bracelet is enough to protect against.</p>

<p><b><u>Monster-find Bracelet, Item-find Bracelet</u></b><br/>Monster-find Bracelet lets you avoid encounters, allowing for more time to regenerate HP.<br/>Item-find Bracelet is useful for planning a more efficient route at the start of a new floor.</p>

<p><b><u>Floating Bracelet</u></b><br/>Pair with Wallpass Bracelet to enhance your range of movement even further.<br/>You also won't need to worry about stepping on traps while this bracelet is equipped.</p>

<p><b><u>Leaping Bracelet</u></b><br/>Lets you warp from room to room instead of having to navigate dark hallways.<br/>It's safer to activate it by walking, instead of stepping in place or swinging your weapon.</p>

<p><b><u>Cursebreak Bracelet</u></b><br/>Protects against Curse Girl (13-20F) and Cursister (21-39F)'s equipment-cursing special attack.<br/>Curse Girl curses an equipped shield, and Cursister curses an equipped weapon or shield.</p>

<p><b><u>Rousing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Protects against Sleep Radish (40-50F)'s Sedating Grass throwing special attack.<br/>Effectively expands your options when choosing an Eradication Scroll target as well.</p>

<p><b><u>Appraiser's Bracelet</u></b><br/><span class="pinkText">Only found from 13F onwards</span> - It's almost guaranteed to appear on 50F in the extension.<br/>Use an Identifier Scroll or Identifier Pot just before clearing the dungeon to take it home.</p>

<h4 id="projectiles-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/arrow.png"/> <img src="../images/other/rock.png"/> Projectiles</h4>

<p><b><u>Porky's Rock</u></b><br/>Projectiles are hard to obtain in meaningful quantities, but they're nice for lighting incenses.<br/>Porky's Rocks can be used to check for incoming monsters even when the hallway bends,<br/>so consider keeping them on hand in favor of other types or rocks or arrows.</p>

<h4 id="staves-&-scrolls-&-grass-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> <img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> <img src="../images/other/grass.png"/> Staves & Scrolls & Grass</h4>

<p><b><u>Identifier Scroll</u></b><br/>Store at least 1 of these inside a pot so that you can take home a specific reward at the end.<br/>If the scroll is blessed and you select an unidentified item, all inventory items will get identified.<br/>There's a small chance for a lucky roll that identifies all items even when it's not blessed.</p>

<p><b><u>Collection Scroll</u></b><br/>Store 1 of these inside a pot for use on 50F if you want to take home an Appraiser's Bracelet.</p>

<p><b><u>Exorcism Scroll</u></b><br/>Cursed items are quite common, so do your best not to waste these scrolls.<br/>It's particularly nice if you can lift a curse from a pot that can be used as storage.</p>

<p><b><u>Monstercall Scroll</u></b><br/>Read these early game when monsters are still manageable to obtain additional items.<br/>Kokatsu Shiren can read them even later on, since Blazing Heat can be used to clear Monster Houses.</p>

<p><b><u>Eradication Scroll</u></b><br/>Pesky Kappa and Vexing Kappa can throw this scroll, making you instantly collapse.<br/>Popular targets on 21-39F include Dragon (15-39F), Nigiri Boss (21-39F), or Pesky Kappa (21-39F).<br/>For targets on 40-50F, the best choice is likely either Sleep Radish (40-50F) or Vexing Kappa (40-50F).<br/>When throwing this scroll at a Bored Kappa type, inflict Sealed, Disguised, or Paralyzed status first.<br/>If Bored Kappa types aren't eradicated and you find an Eradication Scroll you don't plan on using,<br/>be sure to consume it by reading it instead of leaving it on the ground when between 21-50F.</p>

<p><b><u>Confusion Scroll, Slumber Scroll, Jitters Scroll</u></b><br/>Aim to keep one of these on hand for a bad starting room or Summoning Trap accident.<br/>Sweet Potato doesn't seem to be in the item table for this dungeon.</p>

<p><b><u>Paralyzing Staff, Narrow-escape Staff</u></b><br/>Single-target options that are handy when you're struggling to keep up with combat.</p>

<p><b><u>Disguising Staff, Sealing Staff</u></b><br/>Inflicting either status makes it safer to throw an Eradication Scroll at the target.<br/>Don't hesitate to use it against an adjacent Nigiri Boss or such to ensure safety.</p>

<p><b><u>Switching Staff, Vaulting Staff, Burrowing Staff</u></b><br/>Staves used to run away from monsters, or to spend fewer turns on movement.<br/>However, Switching and Vaulting staves can't be used while Sumo status is active.</p>

<p><b><u>Unlucky Staff</u></b><br/>Swing it to significantly weaken most Lv2 or higher monsters in this dungeon.<br/>Nashagga (7-14F), Brodillo (10-14F), Polygon Shaka (15-20F), Nigiri Morph (15-20F), etc.</p>

<p><b><u>Fortune Staff</u></b><br/>Can be used to level up a Pit Mamel (1-3F) into a Cave Mamel for experience points.<br/>It's worth keeping Fortune Staff on hand even if you've gone past the floors where Pit Mamel appears,<br/>since it can be paired with Reflective Incense to hit yourself with magic bullets to rapidly level up.</p>

<p><b><u>Revival Grass</u></b><br/>An extremely important item, since it's easy to collapse from accidents in this dungeon.<br/>Store them in pots, and don't hesitate to read Exorcism Scrolls on these to check for curses.</p>

<p><b><u>Invincible Grass</u></b><br/>Useful as a last-ditch effort when surrounded by monsters.<br/>Blocks Super Gazer (21-39F), Nigiri Boss (21-39F), and Sleep Radish (40-50F) special attacks.</p>

<h4 id="food-&-pots-&-incenses-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/food.png"/> <img src="../images/other/pot.png"/> <img src="../images/other/incense.png"/> Food & Pots & Incenses</h4>

<p><b><u>Grilled Onigiri</u></b><br/>Dragon (15-39F) and Sky Dragon (40-50F) appear for a long time, so this is your main food option.<br/>Eat Special Onigiri and Huge Onigiri before they become grilled if you haven't found a pot.</p>

<p><b><u>Preservation Pot</u></b><br/>Inserted items are exempt from the item auto-identification upon changing floors mechanic,<br/>which means you can insert player-named staves without incrementing the ID'd items count.<br/>Also nice for storing an unidentified item you wish to take home as a reward.</p>

<p><b><u>Identifier Pot</u></b><br/>Useful, but can be risky to carry on Pesky Kappa (21-39F) and Vexing Kappa (40-50F) floors,<br/>since an unidentified item thrown by a kappa can land in the pot and increment the ID'd items count.<br/>Some players like to insert important items to check for curses and break it right away instead.</p>

<p><b><u>Exorcism Pot</u></b><br/>Insert Revival Grass into this pot whenever possible.</p>

<p><b><u>Synthesis Pot, Upgrading Pot</u></b><br/>Fantastic options for upgrading equipment when playing as Normal Shiren or Asuka.<br/>That said, don't count on actually finding either pot during any given adventure.</p>

<p><b><u>Reflective Incense, Evasive Incense</u></b><br/>Reflective Incense can be paired with Fortune Staff to rapidly level up on any floor,<br/>and also counters Gazer (8-20F), Super Gazer (21-39F), Curse Girl (13-20F), Cursister (21-39F), etc.<br/>Evasive Incense counters Sleep Radish (40-50F), but also Lv2-3 Bored Kappa types (21-50F).</p>

<h3 id="farming-techniques" class="heading3Green">Farming Techniques</h3>

<h4 id="cave-mamel-hunting-(1-3f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/1-3.png"/> Cave Mamel Hunting (1-3F)</h4>

Pit Mamel (1-3F) can be leveled up to a Cave Mamel and hunted for 2,000 experience points.<br/>Level up options include items like Fortune Staff, Disguising Staff, Fortune Grass, Jitters Scroll, etc.<br/>Rocks, Knockback Staff, and other items that deal fixed damage can be used to one-shot Cave Mamel.

<h4 id="nigiri-morph-factory-(3-6f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/27-1.png"/> Nigiri Morph Factory (3-6F)</h4>

Nigiri Baby (3-6F) can be used to turn unneeded items into Large Onigiri if you find a shield.<br/>If you find rocks or arrows, turn single rocks or arrows into Large Onigiri instead of the entire stack.<br/>You can do so by stepping on single rocks or arrows and exchanging them with inventory items.<br/>It's worth trying Nigiri Morph Factory even if you don't have a shield - walk around in a large room.<br/>Increasing Max Fullness to 150+ activates Sumo status, which can be quite helpful.<br/>Keep 3\~4 Large Onigiri on hand for the journey after increasing Max Fullness.

<h4 id="greenwalrus-hunting-(16-20f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/29-2.png"/> Greenwalrus Hunting (16-20F)</h4>

Greenwalrus (16-20F) spawns deep enough to possibly drop an Appraiser's Bracelet (13-50F),<br/>so use a Cautious Incense to safely hunt them to increase your odds of finding said bracelet.<br/>However, be careful not to overextend if you're struggling to keep up with other monsters.

# Floor Guide

<p><span class="orangeText">This section assumes that you're attempting to reach the deepest floor instead of returning early.</span></p>

<h3 id="first-clear-(floor-guide)" class="heading3Yellow">First Clear</h3>

### General Advice

The main theme is protecting your inventory against foes capable of stealing or destroying items,<br/>along with finding creative ways to survive stronger monster encounters with weaker equipment.

The newbie "walls" are 5F (Mid Chintala) and 7F (Nashagga) where trading hits usually begins to fail,<br/>unless you've leveled up significantly and/or activated Sumo status using [farming techniques](#farming-techniques).

### 1-3F

Stronger monsters like Chintala (1-3F) and Shagga (1-4F) appear right from the start.<br/>Hallways are dark on all floors, making exploration riskier than in Training Path of Inference.<br/>However, you'll still want to fully explore each floor at least until the end of 6F.

Fortune Staff and Fortune Grass are somewhat common, so try to hunt Cave Mamels to level up.<br/>See [farming related items](#farming-related-(first-clear)) for options to level up a Pit Mamel (1-3F) and defeat a Cave Mamel.

Test items or use the inference mechanic to quickly identify staves and pots (<span class="blueText">Put In</span> Type).<br/>If you find a dungeon shop, attempt to identify items by checking buying or selling prices.<br/>These 2 points are particularly important when aiming for 20F instead of returning early.

### 7-9F

Nashagga (7-14F) attacks 2 times per turn, so item usage is nearly mandatory even with Sumo status.<br/>Unlucky Staff, Sealing Staff, and Water Gun Pot are extremely effective at weakening Nashagga.<br/>The monster table for 7F only has 4 enemies, so Nashagga is particularly common on that floor.

Gazer (8-20F) has a high spawn rate, and hypnotizes you and makes you waste items and turns.<br/>It only has 30 HP so Sumo status or Cyclops Bane can be enough to one-shot it depending on level,<br/>but if you don't have either of those, chip away at its HP using items before it's adjacent.<br/>It's tempting to throw an Eradication Scroll at it, but Dragon (15-20F) might be a better choice.<br/>If you lack a Gazer Shield, it's best to discard incense category items around this point.

Thiefwalrus (8-11F) might end up as an annoying enemy that steals items instead of being farmable.<br/>It's rather risky to attempt to explore a floor in an effort to reclaim a stolen item after it warps away,<br/>so consider shooting a projectile at a wall or placing an unneeded item on the ground as bait.

<h3 id="sacred-tree-extension-(floor-guide)" class="heading3Pink">Sacred Tree Extension</h3>

<h3 id="general-advice" class="heading3Red">General Advice</h3>

Simply clearing the extension isn't difficult, as you can just identify 50 items like the first clear.<br/>However, advancing all the way to 50F and returning with an Appraiser's Bracelet is a different story.<br/>Wallpass, Clairvoyant, and Rousing bracelets are particularly helpful in the extension.

<h4 id="appraiser's-bracelet-(50f)" class="heading4Red"> Appraiser's Bracelet (50F)</h4>

<p><u>Appraiser's Bracelet</u> has an incredibly high chance of being generated on 50F in the extension.<br/>(The chance is assumed to be 100%, but it hasn't actually been datamined at the time of this writing)<br/>In any case, it's a reliable way to obtain said bracelet without resorting to Parallel Play shenanigans,<br/>but there are 2 things you must be aware of when attempting to take this bracelet home as a reward:</p>

<ul>
  <li><span class="pinkText">Auto-identification does not occur when you descend the stairs on 50F.</span></li>
  <li><span class="pinkText">Vexing Kappa (40-50F) can destroy the Appraiser's Bracelet by throwing it at the player.</span></li>
</ul>

Therefore, it's highly recommended to read a Collection Scroll at the start of the floor on 50F,<br/>and save an Identifier Scroll or Identifier Pot to be used exclusively on the Appraiser's Bracelet.<br/>If you're reading an Identifier Scroll, place all other unidentified items down before reading it.

<h4 id="kokatsu-shiren" class="heading4Red"><img src="../images/other/char_kokatsu_shiren.jpg"/> Kokatsu Shiren</h4>

Consider challenging this dungeon as Kokatsu Shiren for an overall easier run.<br/>Kokatsu Shiren doesn't rely on equipment, and can create Special Onigiri for Knowledgeable status.<br/>The only real downside of this character is that it's time consuming to farm via Sun's Blessing,<br/>and you won't be able to equip Monster-find Bracelet or Clairvoyant Bracelet.

<h4 id="meaner-monster-table" class="heading4Red"> Meaner Monster Table</h4>

The monster table of the extension is generally harsher than Training Path of Inference.<br/>Super Gazer and Pesky Kappa appear until 39F instead of exiting the monster table after 29F.<br/>Nigiri Boss and Dragon overlap until 39F instead of Dragon exiting the monster table after 34F.<br/>and Cursister who targets weapons and shields appears until 39F instead of Curse Girl until 35F,<br/>making it harder to swap to secondary equipment when playing as Normal Shiren or Asuka.

Nigiri Boss exits the table after 39F which might sound like an adjustment that makes things easier,<br/>but Soul Reapers will gladly rush you down while you're helplessly asleep thanks to Sleep Radish.<br/>It's also risky to attempt to check for incoming enemies in dark hallways by throwing items,<br/>since both Hoppin' Slugger (40-50F) and Vexing Kappa (40-50F) are around.

<h4 id="think-before-identifying-items" class="heading4Red"> Think Before Identifying Items</h4>

The identification limit of 50 items means you'll want to prioritize identifying <span class="pinkText">useful</span> items.<br/>If you instead simply identify everything, you'll quickly hit 49 ID'd items before reaching 50F,<br/>and will likely be unable to utilize some of the better unidentified items you find later on.<br/>It's also risky to carry an Identifier Pot, since Bored Kappa monsters can throw items into it.

<h4 id="dark-hallways-(1-50f)" class="heading4Red"> Dark Hallways (1-50F)</h4>

All floors in this dungeon, including the extended floors, have dark hallways.<br/>Kokatsu and Tatsumi Shiren have a slight disadvantage compared to Normal Shiren or Asuka,<br/>since neither of the alternate Shirens can equip Monster-find Bracelet or Clairvoyant Bracelet.

<h4 id="thrown-eradication-scrolls-(21-50f)" class="heading4Red"> Thrown Eradication Scrolls (21-50F)</h4>

Eradication Scroll can be found, and Pesky Kappa (21-39F) and Vexing Kappa (40-50F) appear.<br/>This means there's a small chance you'll get hit by a thrown Eradication Scroll if you're unlucky,<br/>resulting in the player character instantly collapsing - it's game over without Revival Grass.<br/>Kokatsu Shiren and Tatsumi Shiren can create their own Revival Grass as a counter.

<h3 id="1-20f" class="heading3Red">1-20F</h3>

This floor range is similar to the [first clear](#first-clear-(floor-guide)), but you'll want to be more conservative with items.<br/>[Hunt Cave Mamels to level up](#cave-mamel-hunting-(1-3f)) on 1-3F, and increase Max Fullness via [Nigiri Morph Factory](#nigiri-morph-factory-(3-6f)) on 3-6F.<br/>Aim to get strong enough to take on Dragon (15-20F) without consuming lots of items each encounter.<br/>Identify unidentified items you find on the ground by utilizing the Inference Mechanic when possible.<br/>You can only identify 50 items total before you're forced to exit the dungeon, so be selective.

<p><u>Unidentified Item Tips</u></p>

- Shops are rare, but they can be used to rule out possibilities based on an item's price.
- If an unidentified item doesn't seem very useful, place it down instead of identifying it.
    - This prevents needlessly incrementing the ID'd items count.
    - Check the list of possibilities to get an idea whether or not the item is useful.
    - Assign a name like "x" before discarding the unidentified item if you deem the item is bad.
- Swing a staff to check its effect, assign a name to it, then insert it into a Preservation Pot.
    - This lets you continue to use the staff while avoiding incrementing the ID'd items count.
- Thin out your unidentified items before advancing in hopes of auto-ID'ing a bracelet or pot.
    - Bracelets and pots are often harder to identify than other categories.
    - <span class="blueText">Storage pots</span> = Preservation, Ordinary, Exorcism, Synthesis, Upgrading, Degrading, Walrus.

<p><u>Kokatsu Shiren & Tatsumi Shiren Tips</u></p>

Prioritize identifying grasses that can be obtained using Sun's Blessing / Rain's Blessing.<br/>However, if a grass isn't useful, assign a name like "x" and recycle it instead of identifying it.

<h3 id="21-39f" class="heading3Red">21-39F</h3>

Dragon, Cursister, Nigiri Boss, Super Gazer, and Pesky Kappa are the only monsters on 21-39F.<br/>They're all tricky in different ways, so consider rushing to the stairs from this point onward.

- Super Gazer
    - Hypnosis can force you to use valuable items, or insert main gear into incenses or pots.
    - Avoid its special attack using Gazer Shield, Reflective Incense, projectiles, staves, etc.
- Nigiri Boss
    - High stats, inflicts Onigiri status (can't use items, negates equipped items for 10 turns).
    - Use an item to stop it from acting when adjacent, or just throw an Eradication Scroll at it.
    - Fire or explosion damage instantly makes you collapse if you have Onigiri status.
- Pesky Kappa
    - Stands on an item → throws it at you when you approach within a 5 tile radius.
    - Pesky Kappa can potentially throw an Eradication Scroll at you (instant collapse).
    - Evasive Incense counters it, and don't leave bad items in rooms you might have to revisit.
- Dragon
    - Dragon's flame instantly makes you collapse if you have Onigiri status when hit.
    - 100% special attack usage rate when lined up at a distance, or through hallway corners.
    - Don't let your guard down even if you have Dragonkiller + Dragon Shield.
- Cursister
    - Curses your equipped weapon or shield, hindering the use of secondary equipment.
    - Cursebreak Bracelet counters it, and having synthesized gear makes it less threatening.

<h3 id="40-50f" class="heading3Red">40-50F</h3>

The monster table is refreshed, and remains static for the remainder of the dungeon (40-50F).<br/>Vexing Kappa, <span class="pinkText">Sleep Radish</span>, and <span class="orangeText">Soul Reaper</span> can still attack you even if you're inside a wall tile.<br/>Evasive Incense is a massive help against Sleep Radish and Vexing Kappa.

Sleep Radish throws Sedating Grass → Poison Grass within a 3 tile radius, making it very deadly.<br/>Soul Reaper has Swift 2 speed, can wall-clip, and detects you from anywhere on the floor.<br/>Vexing Kappa's item throwing range is a 10 tile radius, so it can throw items from off-screen.<br/>Sky Dragon can spew a 20 damage flame when you're in the same room (Nigiri Boss is gone).<br/>Fleefrog has 60 attack power and can pull you from 10 tiles away and strike on the same turn.<br/>Hoppin' Slugger nullifies or reflects magic bullets and thrown items.

<p><u>Appraiser's Bracelet (50F) Tips</u><br/>Sort your items on 49F so that Collection and Identifier scrolls can be used right away on 50F.<br/>Once you descend the stairs on 49F and arrive on 50F, <span class="pinkText">immediately read a Collection Scroll</span>,<br/>then quickly and carefully identify the Appraiser's Bracelet by reading an Identifier Scroll.<br/>It goes without saying, but place all other unidentified items down before reading the scroll,<br/>or else you risk a lucky roll of Identifier Scroll causing you to not take home the bracelet.</p>

If you attract 2 or more unidentified bracelets that list Appraiser's Bracelet as a possibility,<br/>maybe create Parallel Play data and test the bracelets before committing in your real adventure.<br/>(Yes, it's technically cheating, but do you really want to risk having to make a 2nd trip?)
