# Strategy

<h3 id="general-advice-(first-clear)" class="heading3Yellow">General Advice (First Clear)</h3>

The first clear is only 10F, so it's one of the easier no item carry-in dungeons in the game.<br/>Weapons and shields on the ground are low quality, and staves and scrolls can't be found.<br/>Instead, most items that get generated are Gitan bags ranging in value from 100\~1000+ Gitan,<br/>with earlier floors like 1-2F usually generating higher value Gitan bags compared to deeper floors.<br/>So the idea is to stock up on Gitan bags at the start, then rush stairs until you clear the dungeon,<br/>utilizing traps to conserve the number of Gitan bags you throw at enemies when possible.

<h4 id="the-power-of-thrown-gitan-bags" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/gitan.png"/> The power of thrown Gitan bags</h4>

Thrown Gitan bags are powerful projectiles that deal damage equal to <span class="pinkText">20% of the Gitan bag's value</span>.<br/>For example, if you throw a Gitan bag worth 500 Gitan, the target hit by the bag takes 100 damage.<br/>This single item can be enough to give Lv1 Shiren a chance to take out far stronger monsters.

<h4 id="how-to-add-gitan-to-your-inventory" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/gitan.png"/> How to add Gitan bags to your inventory</h4>

1. Hold B and move towards the Gitan bag to step on the Gitan bag without picking it up.
2. Open your inventory, then exchange an inventory item with the Gitan bag underfoot.

<h4 id="froggo-drops-gitan" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/monsters_s/28-1.png"/> Froggo drops Gitan</h4>

Froggo (1-7F) is a Normal type monster that always drops a Gitan bag when defeated.<br/>However, if your Gitan total is currently above 0, a Froggo can steal Gitan, warp, then run away.<br/>※ Gitan bags in your inventory cannot be stolen by Froggos.

<h4 id="firepuff-warning" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/monsters_s/7-1.png"/> Firepuff Warning</h4>

Firepuff (1-3F) is a Dragon and <span class="pinkText">Floating</span> dual-type monster, so it can't be ensnared by traps.<br/>Its special attack deals a fixed 10 damage, so it can be a problem on 1F when Shiren is still Lv1.<br/>It's somewhat common to have to restart until you get an opening where there's a Gitan bag nearby,<br/>which can be stepped on and thrown to instantly defeat a Firepuff.

<h4 id="hoppin'-hitter-&-bored-kappa-warning" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/monsters_s/40-2.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters_s/17-1.png"/> Hoppin' Hitter & Bored Kappa Warning</h4>

Hoppin' Hitter (4-5F) and Bored Kappa (6-10F) have abilities that make them immune to projectiles.<br/>Even worse, Hoppin' Hitter also has a 30% chance to outright reflect a projectile back at the player.<br/>Step on a <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/traps/17.png"/></span> Far-throwing Trap beforehand if you want to hit these monsters with Gitan bags.

### General Advice (Sacred Tree Extension)

The difficulty of the 99F extension is on a completely different level from the 10F first clear,<br/>and is rumored by some players to be the <span class="redText">hardest dungeon</span> (excluding paid DLC) in the game.<br/>To illustrate how harsh this dungeon is compared to Trapper's Secret Path or Peachy Trap Domain:

<div class="centeredTable">

||Cat-Ching's Divine Will|Trapper's Secret Path|Peachy Trap Domain|
|-|-|-|-|
|Trapper Status|<span class="redText">No</span>|<span class="blueText">Yes</span>|<span class="blueText">Yes</span>|
|Behemoth Spawns|<span class="redText">21-95F</span>|<span class="blueText">None</span>|<span class="blueText">None</span>|
|Mixer Spawns|<span class="redText">None</span>|<span class="redText">None</span>|<span class="blueText">16-30 / 60-79F</span>|
|Dark Hallways|<span class="blueText">None</span>|<span class="redText">51-99F</span>|<span class="redText">51-99F</span>|
|Monster Table|<span class="redText">Strong from start.</span>|<span class="blueText">Stabilizes near 30F.</span>|<span class="redText">Strong from start.</span>|

</div>

Items are low quality, so you still have to focus on evasive play while relying on Gitan or traps.<br/>Meanwhile, the monster table ramps up in power at one of the fastest paces among all dungeons.<br/>In addition, threats that are immune to traps or have swift speed or other strong abilities appear:

- Swift - Great Hen (21-35F / 50-69F), Dragon Pit (50-69F), Miracle Hen (80-99F).
- Trap-immune - Firepuff (1-3F), Blazepuff (30-40F), Pyrepuff (50-69F), Dragon Pit (50-69F).
- Ranged - Thrashead (23-49F), Megahead (72-99F), Archdragon (70-89F), Abyss Dragon (90-99F).
- Other - N'daloon (61-74F), Lv3 Monks (35-49F), Lv4 Monks (85-99F), Swordmaster (90-99F), etc.

To make matters worse, staves and scrolls are never found outside of making a Behemoth Gate vanish.<br/>Players will be challenged to conserve items and find ways to restock their inventory under pressure.<br/>That said, rewards left behind by Behemoth Gates and equipment luck influences the difficulty a lot.<br/>All floors have visible hallways, so it's not super dire without Monster-find and Item-find bracelets.<br/>Tatsumi Shiren might have the easiest time due to flowing water becoming an escape path.

<h4 id="sumo-status-tips" class="heading4"><img src="../images/icons/19.png"/> Sumo Status Tips</h4>

Rotting and Blast traps can be used to increase Max Fullness to 200 and maintain Sumo status.<br/>However, there are pros and cons to Sumo status, so going for it comes down to player preference:

- (<span class="blueText">+</span>) Destroy dangerous traps like Rage Trap simply by stepping on them.
- (<span class="blueText">+</span>) The x1.5 damage multiplier during Sumo status also applies to arrows.
- (<span class="blueText">+</span>) The ability to dig out wall tiles lets you collect arrows more easily.
    - Thrown items land 10 tiles ahead, so create a 10-tile line to the arrow trap from any direction.
- (<span class="blueText">+</span>) Digging through walls makes it easier to make a Behemoth Gate vanish for a reward.
- (<span class="redText">−</span>) Far-throwing Trap can't be used to counter Behemoth Monsters or a line of enemies.
- (<span class="redText">−</span>) Floor-exiting Trap can't be used to quickly skip to the next floor.
- (<span class="redText">−</span>) Monster Houses can end the run if you don't have Invincible Grass or Swift Grass.

<h4 id="behemoth-tips" class="heading4"><img src="../images/icons/24.png"/> Behemoth Tips</h4>

A fixed type of Behemoth Monster has a chance to appear every 5 floors between 21-95F.<br/>Rocks can't be found, so Far-throwing Trap is often needed to one-shot Behemoth Monsters.<br/>B-Atrocious Kappa (71-75F) makes it hard to clear the <span class="blueText">Complete without collapsing</span> restriction.<br/>The Behemoth Monster families include Pumphantasm, Bored Kappa, Gazer, and Hoppin' Batter:

- 21-25F - Gazer
- 26-30F - Super Gazer
- 31-35F - <span class="orangeText">Pumphantasm</span>
- 36-40F - Bored Kappa
- 41-45F - Hoppin' Batter
- 46-50F - <span class="orangeText">Pumpanshee</span>
- 51-55F - <span class="redText">Pesky Kappa</span>
- 56-60F - <span class="redText">Vexing Kappa</span>
- 61-65F - Hoppin' Hitter
- 66-70F - <span class="orangeText">Pumplich</span>
- 71-75F - <span class="redText">Atrocious Kappa</span>
- 76-80F - Hyper Gazer
- 81-85F - <span class="redText">Ultra Gazer</span>
- 86-90F - <span class="orangeText">Pumplord</span>
- 91-95F - Hoppin' Slugger

<h3 id="trap-notes" class="heading3Purple">Trap Notes</h3>

<h4 id="spinning-/-sleep-/-blinding-/-sluggish-/-delusion-/-onigiri" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/11.png"/> Spinning <img src="../images/traps/12.png"/> Sleep <img src="../images/traps/14.png"/> Blinding <img src="../images/traps/18.png"/> Sluggish <img src="../images/traps/15.png"/> Delusion <img src="../images/traps/16.png"/> Onigiri</h4>

Traps that inflict a negative status condition, with Delusion Trap being the most powerful.<br/>Monsters with Deluded status won't attack Shiren, and will instead run away from him forever.<br/>The second best trap is Blinding Trap, since its effect similarly never wears off for monsters.<br/>Sleep and Onigiri have short durations, so only use it against enemies you can defeat quickly.

<h4 id="spring" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/8.png"/> Spring</h4>

Step on this when you're being chased to warp to a different location on the current floor.<br/>Basically the same as Leaping Grass, except you can't carry the Spring Trap around.

<h4 id="rotting" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/21.png"/> Rotting</h4>

Monsters who step on this trap are instantly defeated and transform into a Rotten Onigiri.<br/>Rotten Onigiri can be thrown at a monster to inflict a negative status condition.<br/>Blast Trap or Firepuff (1-3F) can be used to turn Rotten Onigiri into Grilled Onigiri.

<h4 id="blast-/-explosion" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/6.png"/> Blast <img src="../images/traps/7.png"/> Explosion</h4>

Any monsters caught in the blast that occurs will instantly collapse.<br/>It's possible to step on it when a Floating type monster is adjacent to vaporize the monster.<br/>In this game, Shiren won't collapse even if his current HP is 1 when he steps on this trap.

<h4 id="far-throwing" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/17.png"/> Far-throwing</h4>

Grants Far-throwing Status, which makes thrown items pierce through walls and creatures.<br/>Stepping on this trap lets you throw Gitan against Hoppin' Hitter (4-5F) and Bored Kappa (6-10F).<br/>Just be aware that you won't be able to recover thrown Gitan that misses its target.

<h4 id="poison-arrow-/-wooden-arrow" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/3.png"/> Poison Arrow <img src="../images/traps/2.png"/> Wooden Arrow</h4>

A single Poison Arrow reduces enemy attack power by about 50%.<br/>It can help you withstand more hits while running away, but Shiren's attack power is too low<br/>to keep up with stronger enemies later on either way, so don't bother collecting tons of arrows.<br/>Wooden Arrows mostly stop being useful after 4F or so.

<h4 id="rust" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/20.png"/> Rust</h4>

Lowers a monster's defense, and reduces Minotaur (5-10F) and Lashagga (7-10F)'s attack power.<br/>Same effect as Water Gun Pot against monsters that wield weapons, and also seals Zapdon family.<br/>A monster that has Onigiri status who steps on a Rust Trap <span class="redText">won't</span> instantly collapse.

<h4 id="hunger-/-removal" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/10.png"/> Hunger <img src="../images/traps/19.png"/> Removal</h4>

Lowers a monster's attack power (Hunger Trap), or attack power + defense (Removal Trap).

<h4 id="floor-exiting" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/29.png"/> Floor-exiting</h4>

Step on this to instantly advance to the next floor - hurry over to it before a monster steps on it.<br/>This dungeon has ascending stairs, so Pitfall Traps are never generated.

<h4 id="trap-setting" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/31.png"/> Trap-setting</h4>

Step on this to increase the number of traps on the ground.<br/>Shiren can see traps from the start, so there's not much downside to stepping on it.

<h4 id="summoning-/-rage" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/24.png"/> Summoning <img src="../images/traps/26.png"/> Rage</h4>

These 2 traps offer zero advantages for you when activated - avoid having monsters step on them.<br/>Some players activate Summoning Traps on 3F to spawn extra Cave Mamels (3-4F) or Froggos (1-7F).<br/>Summoning Traps on 4F are riskier, since Hoppin' Hitter (4-5F) and Zapdon (4-10F) can appear.

<h3 id="important-items-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Blue">Important Items (Sacred Tree Extension)</h3>

Equipment is never found cursed in this dungeon, so it's safe to identify by equipping items.<br/>Blue Sacred gear can rarely be found, and Synthesis Pot can be obtained from a Behemoth Gate.

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/weapon/15.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/10.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>Dragonkiller, Dragon Shield</u></b><br/>Dragon Shield halves damage from Archdragon (70-89F) and Abyss Dragon (90-99F).<br/>Dragonkiller's resonance with Dragon Shield boosts Anti-Dragon's multiplier (x1.5 → x2.5),<br/>making it easier to slay Dragon types like Pyrepuff (50-69F) and Dragon Pit (50-69F).</p>

<p><b><u>Primal Axe</u></b><br/>Nice against Great Hen (21-35F / 50-69F) and Miracle Hen (80-99F).</p>

<p><b><u>Cyclops Bane</u></b><br/>Helpful against Thrashead (23-49F) and Megahead (72-99F).</p>

<p><b><u>Shield of Sating</u></b><br/>Less need to carry lots of food means more inventory space for other items.<br/>Also essentially doubles the duration that Shiren can stay in Sumo status.</p>

<p><b><u>Watchful Shield, Counter Shield</u></b><br/>Most foes lack range so they approach until adjacent and use normal attacks in this dungeon.<br/>Therefore, prioritize these shields over shields that counter specific special attacks.</p>

<p><b><u>Wooden Mallet</u></b><br/>Consider using even a Wooden Mallet if you find a sacred one with good runes.<br/>Traps are visible, so you're unlikely to lose upgrade value from breaking traps.</p>

<h4 id="bracelets-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/bracelet.png"/> Bracelets</h4>

<p><b><u>Rapid-fire Bracelet</u></b><br/>Boosts projectile accuracy (84% → 91%), and lets you shoot 2 projectiles in 1 turn.<br/>Shoot 2 Poison Arrows in 1 turn, throw a Gitan bag with greater accuracy, etc.</p>

<p><b><u>Cleansing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Nullifies the Strength -3 effect when eating Rotten Onigiri.<br/>You might not have time to turn Rotten Onigiri into Grilled Onigiri mid~late game.</p>

<p><b><u>Strength Bracelet</u></b><br/>One of the more practical bracelets to keep equipped in this dungeon,<br/>since bracelets that block status conditions or special attacks aren't very necessary.</p>

<h4 id="projectiles-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/arrow.png"/> <img src="../images/other/gitan.png"/> Projectiles</h4>

<p><b><u>Poison Arrow</u></b><br/>A single Poison Arrow reduces a monster's attack power by 50%.<br/>Collect from Poison Arrow Traps, and keep 2 stacks of 99 Poison Arrows on hand at all times.<br/>None of the monsters in the extension are immune to projectiles aside from Behemoths.</p>

<p><b><u>Wooden Arrow</u></b><br/>Step on a Far-throwing Trap and shoot these to slay Behemoth Monsters (21-95F).<br/>Poison has diminishing returns, so shoot 1 Poison Arrow → Wooden Arrows to chip away at foes.</p>

<p><b><u>Silver Arrow</u></b><br/>Rock category items can't be found, so carry these to counter Behemoth Monsters (21-95F).</p>

<p><b><u>Gitan</u></b><br/>Thrown Gitan (damage = 20% of value) is the quickest way to deal with powerful monsters.<br/>1,000 Gitan (200 damage) handles most foes, but some like Pyrepuff or Lv4 Monks have 255~280 HP.<br/>The value of Gitan bags found on the ground can be too low to one-shot stronger enemies,<br/>so hunt Froggo (1-7F), Froggucchi (12-39F), and B-Ultra Gazer (81-85F) for higher value Gitan bags.</p>

<h4 id="staves-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> Staves</h4>

<p><span class="orangeText">Staves are seemingly never found in this dungeon, even in the 99F extension.</span></p>

<h4 id="scrolls-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> Scrolls</h4>

<p><span class="orangeText">Scrolls are seemingly only found as a reward for making a Behemoth Gate vanish in this dungeon.</span></p>

<p><b><u>Blank Scroll</u></b><br/>Occasionally obtained from making a Behemoth Gate (21-95F) vanish.<br/>Eradicate Archdragon, Expulsion a Monster House, Silverpurge a good Blue Sacred item, etc.</p>

<h4 id="grass-&-food-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/grass.png"/> <img src="../images/other/food.png"/> Grass & Food</h4>

<p><b><u>Leaping Grass</u></b><br/>Keep at least 1 on hand at all times to escape from a surprise Monster House.<br/>If you're going with Sumo status, carry Swift Grass and/or Invincible Grass instead.<br/>Tatsumi Shiren can simply use an ability if you have enough Fullness.</p>

<p><b><u>Revival Grass</u></b><br/>Stock as many as you can to survive the Lv2-4 Behemoth Bored Kappa floors.<br/>Keep a balanced inventory of Revival, Invincible, Swift, and Leaping grass.</p>

<p><b><u>Swift Grass, Invincible Grass</u></b><br/>Survival options when sandwiched in a hallway or surrounded by enemies.</p>

<p><b><u>Grilled Onigiri</u></b><br/>Main food source that replenishes 75 Fullness and restores 40 HP.<br/>Max Fullness of 196+ lets you eat these without any Fullness going to waste during Sumo status.</p>

<p><b><u>Rotten Onigiri</u></b><br/>Throw at a monster to inflict a negative status condition and conserve Gitan bags.</p>

<p><b><u>Onigiri, Large Onigiri</u></b><br/>Replenish Fullness by eating Grilled Onigiri before eating these 2 to raise Max Fullness.<br/>Onigiri increases Max Fullness by 3, and Large Onigiri by 4.</p>

<h4 id="pots-&-incenses-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/pot.png"/> <img src="../images/other/incense.png"/> Pots & Incenses</h4>

<p><b><u>Preservation Pot</u></b><br/>Prioritize duplicating these pots when you encounter Bouncy Wall Rooms.<br/>There are many items you want to carry - food, synthesis ingredients, Revival Grass, and more.</p>

<p><b><u>Cashing Pot</u></b><br/>Insert unneeded equipment to restock high value Gitan bags.<br/>Some players skip hoping to find a Synthesis Pot in favor of obtaining more Gitan bags.<br/>Can also be used as storage for Gitan bags dropped by Froggucchi (12-39F).</p>

<p><b><u>Rejuvenation Pot</u></b><br/>Also occasionally obtained from making a Behemoth Gate (21-95F) vanish.<br/>Use it to recover from Okina Monk related accidents, Archdragon / Abyss Dragon flames, etc.</p>

<p><b><u>Water Gun Pot</u></b><br/>Reduces Attack Power by 8 stages against weapon-wielding monsters,<br/>including Lashagga (7-20F), Megataur (21-35F), Bashagga (36-69F), and Swordmaster (90-99F).</p>

<p><b><u>Synthesis Pot</u></b><br/>Occasionally obtained from making a Behemoth Gate (21-95F) vanish.<br/>Powerful item when playing as Normal Shiren or Asuka, but middling as Kokatsu or Tatsumi Shiren.<br/><span class="pinkText">Check to make sure you don't have Far-throwing status before throwing it at a wall.</span></p>

<p><b><u>Upgrading Pot</u></b><br/>Found on the ground, but incredibly rare - you'd be lucky to see 1 per 99F run.<br/>Also hard to utilize past early~mid game, so just don't bother including it in your strategy.<br/><span class="pinkText">Check to make sure you don't have Far-throwing status before throwing it at a wall.</span></p>

<p><b><u>Heat-ban Incense</u></b><br/>Helps against Pyrepuff (50-69F), Archdragon (70-89F), and Abyss Dragon (90-99F).<br/>However, Dragon Shield or Eradication (Blank) Scroll are the best counters for the Dragon family.</p>

<p><b><u>Blurry Incense</u></b><br/>Adds some safety when rushing to the stairs or a Floor-exiting Trap.<br/>Handy on Archdragon (70-89F) and Abyss Dragon (90-99F) floors, flowing water floors, and so on.<br/>Of course, it can also be used to survive a Monster House, so never discard these.</p>

<p><b><u>Evasive Incense</u></b><br/>Counters B-Atrocious Kappa (71-75F), who throws items at you from anywhere on the floor.<br/>Plays poorly with thrown Gitan, so go ahead and swap it for a better item past 75F.</p>

# Floor Guide

<h3 id="first-clear" class="heading3Yellow">First Clear</h3>

### General Advice

Hallways are visible on all floors, so make heavy use of the <span class="blueText">Look Around</span> command in rooms.<br/>Don't dash - you can quickly end up in a bad situation even with the 1 tile gap setting enabled.<br/>Look for chances to lure non-Floating type monsters onto traps to conserve Gitan bags and items.<br/>Step on a Far-throwing Trap to throw Gitan bags at Hoppin' Hitter (4-5F) and Bored Kappa (6-10F).<br/>Thrown items can miss, so it's best to throw Gitan from a distance instead of when adjacent.

Keep your Gitan total at 0 to ensure Froggo (1-7F) drops a high value Gitan bag when slain.<br/>Hunt every single Froggo you encounter for Gitan drops, unless you need to run from a strong foe.<br/>Thrown Gitan bags are very important to surviving encounters near the end of the first clear.<br/>The value of Gitan bags tends to be higher early on, and decreases as you descend deeper.

Consider rushing to the stairs starting on 5F (Minotaur appears) or 8F (Froggo stops spawning).<br/>The first clear is only 10F, so don't go overboard with lingering / farming techniques for exp or items.

### 1-2F

Shagga (1-3F) looks strong, but is easily slain in a 1 vs 1 fight by retreating to regen HP.<br/><span class="redText">Firepuff (1-3F)</span> is the most dangerous enemy in this floor range, contrary to its cute looks.<br/>It takes \~4 hits for a Lv1 Shiren to defeat Firepuff, while Firepuff's flame deals 10 damage,<br/>so you'll need to either throw Gitan at it, or have Shiren step on a Blast Trap to one-shot it.<br/>Avoid entering longer hallways if you need to run away from a Firepuff.

Don't pick up Gitan normally - Step on it by holding B, then exchange it with an inventory item.<br/>This lets you carry the Gitan bag as an item, which can be thrown as a powerful projectile.<br/>A thrown Gitan bag deals damage equal to <span class="pinkText">20% of the Gitan bag's value</span> to the target monster.<br/>Staying at 0 Gitan also lets you hunt Froggo (1-7F) for more Gitan bags without being stolen from.<br/>When throwing Gitan bags, save higher value bags for later floors instead of using them early on.

Once you've leveled up a bit, lure Mamels and Shaggas onto Rotting Traps to make Rotten Onigiri.<br/>Rotten Onigiri turns into HP restoring Grilled Onigiri when Shiren gets hit by Firepuff's flame.<br/>Firepuff has a 100% special attack usage rate through hallway corners.

Collect Poison Arrows (reduces enemy attack power by \~50%) if you find a Poison Arrow Trap.<br/>Linger until the wind blows on 1-2F to level up and obtain Gitan bags and Grilled Onigiri.<br/>Lastly, Bronze Sword and Wooden Shield have a high chance of being generated between 1-5F.<br/>Weapons and shields are otherwise very rare, so do your best to explore and find these 2 items.<br/>However, keep in mind that a Monsterizing Trap can end up destroying the gear if you're unlucky.

### 3F

Cave Mamel (3-4F) appears as a somewhat rare encounter.<br/>Normal attacks only deal 1 damage to it, but it can be insta-slain by any thrown Gitan bag.<br/>Cave Mamel provides 2,000 experience points, so it's best to hunt any that you encounter.<br/>It's still generally pretty safe to linger until the wind blows on this floor.

Soldier Ant (3-5F) digs out wall tiles instead of chasing Shiren, so it's mostly harmless.<br/>However, Soldier Ant will attack if you become adjacent to it, including through hallway corners.<br/>Soldier Ant requires <span class="oceanBlueText">250+</span> Gitan to one-shot.

### 4-5F

Hoppin' Hitter (4-5F) reflects or nullifies projectiles like arrows or thrown Gitan bags.<br/>The easiest counter is to step on a Far-throwing Trap to gain Far-throwing Status, then throw Gitan,<br/>since Hoppin' Hitter can't reflect or nullify projectiles with piercing properties.

Zapdon (4-10F) has Slow action speed but high attack power and defensive stats.<br/>It's best to ignore it since hitting it 3 times activates a 25 damage lightning counter attack.<br/>Zapdon requires <span class="oceanBlueText">400+</span> Gitan to one-shot.

Minotaur (5-10F) and Metalhead (5-10F) both hit hard, so use ranged attacks before they're adjacent.<br/>Keep in mind that Metalhead can attack from up to 2 tiles away, including through corners.<br/>Minotaur requires <span class="oceanBlueText">350+</span> Gitan to one-shot, and Metalhead <span class="oceanBlueText">330+</span> Gitan.

### 6F

This is the only floor with a "Shuffle Dungeon" style map generation in this dungeon.<br/>The map is chosen from a group of predetermined patterns instead of being randomly generated.

New threats include Bored Kappa (6-10F) and Master Hen (6-10F).<br/>Bored Kappa is immune to projectiles that lack piercing properties, similar to Hoppin' Hitter.<br/>Master Hen is a brawler style monster like Minotaur, but provides 400 experience points,<br/>so they're a nice target to hunt to level up if you weren't able to hunt a Cave Mamel earlier.<br/>Master Hen requires <span class="oceanBlueText">350+</span> Gitan to one-shot.

Gitan bags found on the ground decrease in value to 300 or less from around this point.<br/>The final floor with Froggo is 7F, so combined with strong Dragon type monsters appearing soon,<br/>it's recommended to rush to the stairs to conserve Gitan bags starting from 7F or 8F.

Floor-exiting Traps that can be stepped on to advance to the next floor appear from this point.<br/>If your stock of Gitan bags and Rotten Onigiri looks good, feel free to step on said traps.

### 7-10F

Lashagga (7-10F) attacks 3 times per turn when adjacent, nearly guaranteeing a collapse.<br/>Dragon (8-10F) has high offensive and defensive stats, and spews 20 damage fire in a straight line.<br/>Lashagga requires <span class="oceanBlueText">495+</span> Gitan to one-shot, Dragon <span class="oceanBlueText">600+ Gitan</span>, and Corporal Ant <span class="oceanBlueText">500+</span> Gitan.

8-10F often features water tiles, so flowing water and rafts can be generated on occasion.<br/>Avoid going against the flow of water if possible, and use the <span class="blueText">Look Around</span> command a lot.<br/>The goal is close, so take things one step at a time and do your best!

Hallways are still always visible, so make heavy use of the <span class="blueText">Look Around</span> command in rooms.<br/><span class="pinkText">Don't dash</span> - Monsters with Swift speed and threats that can potentially one-shot you appear later on.<br/>Monster Houses are very rare, but the wall-break style can be generated, so carry 1-2 Leaping Grass.<br/>Beware of Swift monsters (21-35F / 50-69F / 80-99F), Monks (35-49F / 85-99F), N'daloon (61-74F).<br/>Try for a Blank Scroll from a Behemoth Gate before Archdragon (70-89F) and Abyss Dragon (90-99F).<br/>Froggo (1-7F), Froggucchi (12-39F), and B-Ultra Gazer (81-85F) can be hunted to restock Gitan bags.<br/>Utilize Floor-exiting Traps when possible, and don't try to check every room later on.<br/>
