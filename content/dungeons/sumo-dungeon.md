# Strategy

<h3 id="general-advice" class="heading3Yellow">General Advice (First Clear & Sacred Tree Extension)</h3>

Dungeon where you're expected to progress while managing Fullness and utilizing Sumo status.<br/>Sumo Onigiri is the only food item found on the ground, and Feeding Traps are also pretty common.<br/>Rotting Trap, Onigiri Scroll, and fire & explosion sources appear, so other onigiri items can be obtained.

Hidden items can appear in wall tiles, incentivizing digging out walls during Sumo status.<br/>Wishing Shrines and Gitan Vaults can also be generated, generally making it easier to amass items.<br/>It's easy among dungeons that allow bringing items, making it a great first stop after clearing the story.<br/>Many players like to challenge the first clear without bringing any items into the dungeon.

Sumo status is an extremely powerful mechanic that can be utilized in other postgame dungeons,<br/>so it's great to spend time here to learn how it works, along with practicing Fullness management.<br/>An effective strategy is to activate Sumo status in harder floor ranges, and disable it in easier ones.<br/>However, said strategy is tricky to apply to this dungeon due to the dungeon's gimmick.

<h4 id="sumo-status" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/icons/19.png"/> Sumo Status</h4>

Occurs when Max Fullness reaches 150, and is canceled when Fullness depletes to 120.<br/>Sumo status boosts the player's attack power by 50%, and also increases max HP by 50.<br/>In addition, Shiren can also dig through wall tiles without penalty, and becomes immune to traps.<br/>However, fullness depletion is doubled, and movement effects like Vaulting Staff are nullified,<br/>so Sumo status isn't strictly all positive effects.

<h4 id="sumo-onigiri" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/other/food.png"/> Sumo Onigiri</h4>

Nigiri Baby does not appear, so Sumo Onigiri is likely going to be your only food source.<br/>It's hard to maintain Sumo status if you don't bring items like Peach Club or Shield of Sating,<br/>so aim to only activate Sumo status when you need the additional HP and attack power.<br/>Beware of monster special attacks that cause onigiri to become grilled or rotten.

<h4 id="behemoth-monsters-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4"><img src="../images/icons/24.png"/> Behemoth Monsters (Sacred Tree Extension)</h4>

Behemoth Gates have a chance to spawn on 21-95F in the 99F Sacred Tree Mode extension.<br/>However, only 1 type of Behemoth appears, and the specific monster changes every 5 floors.<br/>Up to 3 Behemoth Monsters can spawn at once, or up to 2 for B-Soul Reaper (91-95F).<br/>B-Soul Reaper also despawns faster than other Behemoths - roughly 12 turns.

<h3 id="important-items" class="heading3Purple">Important Items (First Clear & Sacred Tree Extension)</h3>

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/weapon/19.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/29.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>Drain Slayer</u></b><br/>Sword that boosts damage dealt to Draining type monsters (x1.5 multiplier).<br/>Particularly good for Scorpion and Polygon Spinna families in the Sacred Tree Extension.</p>

<p><b><u>Shield of Sating</u></b><br/>Halves hunger rate while equipped (1 Fullness / 10 Turns → 1 Fullness / 20 Turns).<br/>Shiren's Sumo status is x2.0 hunger, so this shield sets Sumo status hunger to normal speed.</p>

<h4 id="bracelets" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/bracelet.png"/> Bracelets</h4>

<p><b><u>Item-find Bracelet, Clairvoyant Bracelet</u></b><br/>Reveals where items are buried inside wall tiles.</p>

<p><b><u>Monster-find Bracelet, Clairvoyant Bracelet</u></b><br/>Reveals where monsters are located on the current floor - Nice for dark hallway (15-30F) floors.</p>

<p><b><u>Trapper's Bracelet (Carry-In)</u></b><br/>Reveals hidden traps, and lets you pick up traps as items & set traps to ensnare monsters.<br/>Great for locating Feeding Traps and avoiding Starvation Traps in particular.</p>

<h4 id="projectiles-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/arrow.png"/> <img src="../images/other/rock.png"/> Projectiles</h4>

<p><b><u>Wooden Arrow, Iron Arrow</u></b><br/>Sumo status's x1.5 damage multiplier also applies to arrows shot by the player.<br/>Strength Grass is common, so the combo of high strength + Sumo status makes arrows potent.<br/>Arrows can only be collected from Bowboy & Crossbowboy (6F) during the first clear,<br/>but Baby Tank (23-49F) appears for a long time during the Sacred Tree Extension.</p>

<p><b><u>Poison Arrow</u></b><br/>A single Poison Arrow reduces a monster's attack power by 50%.<br/>However, it's quite hard to collect these in this dungeon since Karakuroid doesn't appear at all.<br/>Your only realistic option is to check for traps using normal attacks if Sumo status is active,<br/>since Sumo status destroys traps you step on - all said, it's likely not worth the effort.</p>

<p><b><u>Rock, Porky's Rock</u></b><br/>A single rock type item one-shots a Ghost Samurai (6F) or Behemoth Monster (21-95F).</p>

<h4 id="other-items" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/food.png"/> <img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> <img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> Other Items</h4>

<p><b><u>Collection Scroll, Burrowing Staff</u></b><br/>Makes it easier to obtain items that are hidden inside wall tiles.<br/>It's probably best to save these for when you find a shop, Preservation Pot, or Identifier Pot.</p>

<p><b><u>Mapping Scroll</u></b><br/>Pretty common, and great for dark hallway (15-30F) floors in particular.<br/>If you're attempting the first clear, it's recommended to save 2 Mapping Scrolls for 19-20F.</p>

<p><b><u>Invincible Grass, Confusion Scroll, Jitters Scroll</u></b><br/>Items that can be used in Monster Houses even when Sumo status is active.<br/>Confusion Scroll is best for single room MHs, while Invincible Grass is great for pop-up MHs.</p>

<p><b><u>Paralyzing Staff, Narrow-escape Staff</u></b><br/>Single-target options that can be used even when Sumo status is active.<br/>Narrow-escape Staff can be combined with Monster-find Bracelet to locate the stairs.</p>

<h4 id="traps-(items)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/traps/32.png"/> <img src="../images/traps/33.png"/> Traps</h4>

<p><b><u>Feeding Trap</u></b><br/><span class="blueText">Positive Trap</span> - Increases Fullness and Max Fullness by 25, then breaks.<br/>Stops generating around 30F or so? - don't forget to cancel Sumo status before stepping on it!</p>

<p><b><u>Starvation Trap</u></b><br/><span class="redText">Negative Trap</span> - Decreases Fullness and Max Fullness by 25, then breaks.<br/>It's safe to step on this when Sumo status is active, since the trap will simply be destroyed.</p>

# Floor Guide

<h3 id="first-clear" class="heading3Yellow">First Clear</h3>

### General Advice

<p><span class="orangeText">This section assumes that the player isn't bringing any items into the dungeon.</span><br/>The first clear is like a slightly harder version of Serpentcoil Island condensed to 20 floors.<br/>You'll burn through Sumo Onigiri very quickly if you try to explore while Sumo status is active,<br/>so it's best to only rely on Sumo status when rushing to the stairs or when fighting stronger foes.<br/>Some players begin rushing to the stairs starting from 10F.</p>

Try to claim items buried in walls if you have Fullness or digging-related items to spare.<br/>Use items like Item-find Bracelet, Clairvoyant Bracelet, or Mapping Scroll to locate buried items.<br/>Retrieving options include Collection Scroll, Burrowing Staff, Wallpass Bracelet, Pumphantasm Bun, etc.<br/>Sumo status can also be used, but Fullness depletes quickly without <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/shield/29.png"/></span> Sating (shield rune).

Enemy lineup is based on Serpentcoil Island 1F (Sozoro Bay) - Stay in normal form.<br/>Linger until the wind blows for experience points and item drops, and check for <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/traps/32.png"/></span> Feeding Traps,<br/>which can be intentionally stepped on to increase the player's Max Fullness by 25.

### 18-20F

Enemy lineup is based on Serpentcoil Island 25-26F (Mount Jatou), <b>dark hallways: 18-20F</b>.<br/>Aim to remain in Sumo status on these last 3 floors, and focus on finding the stairs quickly.<br/>Mapping Scroll is a significant help, along with arrows in hallways to check for incoming foes.

Pesky Kappa (18-20F) throws ground items at you when you get within a 5 tile radius.<br/>Porko (18-20F) throws a Porky's Rock within a 5 tile radius that deals 20 damage.<br/>Dartingfrog (18-20F) pulls you closer from up to 5 tiles ahead and attacks on the same turn.<br/>Daze Radish (20F) throws Confusion Grass → Poison Grass within a 2 tile radius.

Hunt a Menbell (19-20F) for the Monster Notebook if desired, as it's rare to see it spawn naturally.<br/>However, you can also unlock its notebook entry by eating a Menbell Bun purchased in Shukuba Beach.<br/>You might be tempted to hunt Greenwalrus (19-20F) for items or use Mixer (18-20F) for synthesis,<br/>but it's pointless to do so during the first clear since the goal is so close.

<h3 id="sacred-tree-extension" class="heading3Pink">Sacred Tree Extension</h3>

<h3 id="general-advice-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Red">General Advice</h3>

Challenging the extension without bringing items is still doable, but can be quite spicy.<br/>You'll probably struggle to fight late game monsters even with the help of Sumo status.<br/><span class="pinkText">Synthesize gear on Mixer (18-23F) floors</span>, and restock arrows on Baby Tank (23-49F) floors.<br/>Narrow-escape Staff can be swung at a Hoppin' Slammer (50-99F) to warp to the stairs.

Hallways are only dark between 15-30F, and the remainder of the dungeon has visible hallways.<br/>Monster Houses seem to be more common than some other dungeons, including single room MHs.<br/>Items like Confusion Scroll and Blurry Incense are key to surviving them during Sumo status.<br/>Throw Eradication Scrolls at Baby Tank (23-49F) → Pop Tanks (50-99F), or Scorpions (24-99F).<br/>Prioritize Scorpions if you can't one-shot them and don't have a Cleansing Bracelet.

The monster table for the extension focuses on 4 main types of threats on 24-99F:

- Polygon Spinna family's Fullness / Max Fullness decrease.
    - Polygon Shaka (24-49F, -5 Fullness) → Polygon Singa (50-99F, -7 Max Fullness).
- Scorpion family's Strength / Max Strength decrease.
    - Vile Scorpion (24-49F, -2 Strength) → Terror Scorpion (50-99F, -1 Max Strength).
- Hoppin' Batter family's magic bullet & projectile reflect.
    - Hoppin' Slugger (24-49F) → Hoppin' Slammer (50-99F).
- Monsters that can grill or rot Sumo Onigiri that aren't in pots.
    - Sky Dragon (22-50F), Lv2-4 Tanks (50-99F), Detonachin (60-75F), Fire Ninja B (76-90F), etc.

Also, a fixed type of behemoth monster has a chance to appear every 5 floors between 21-95F.<br/>The max number of behemoths is 3 per floor, aside from B-Soul Reaper which has a max of 2.<br/>Consider hunting Mini Tanks (50-75F) for Silver Arrow drops to counter notorious Behemoths,<br/>and don't forget that Wall-less Scroll can be used to instantly make all Behemoths vanish.

<h3 id="1-23f" class="heading3Red">1-23F</h3>

<p><span class="purpleText2">Behemoth Gates have a chance of being generated on 21-23F.</span><br/>Behemoths only includes Dragon (21-25F), so Behemoth Monsters are not a huge threat yet.<br/>As for regular monsters, this floor range mostly follows a condensed version of Serpentcoil Island.<br/><b>Hallways are only dark between 15-30F</b> - The rest of the dungeon has visible hallways.</p>

The <a href="#first-clear">strategy for the first clear</a> can be used, but don't skip synthesizing with <span class="blueText">Mixers (18-23F)</span>.<br/>Daze Radish (20-23F) overlaps with Mixer, so aim to synthesize <span class="runeIcon"><img src="../images/rune/weapon/18.png"/></span> Confusing into your weapon.<br/>The extension goes until 99F, so don't eat all of your Sumo Onigiri near the end of this floor range.<br/>Cleansing Bracelet is valuable in this dungeon thanks to the prevalence of Scorpion family monsters,<br/>and watch for Preservation Pots, which can be used to prevent Sumo Onigiri from becoming grilled.

Don't forget to collect arrows on 6F since Bowboy family monsters and Bored Kappa overlap.<br/>Depending on item luck, you might be able to restock arrows on Baby Tank (23-49F) floors too.<br/>Some players like to linger on Minotaur (10-12F) floors for an Axe of the Minotaur drop,<br/>but the weapon is quite rare, so don't expect it to drop even if you linger on all 3 floors.
