Mystery Dungeon where the player must battle and win against 4 bosses in a row on the last 4 floors.<br/>The first 20 floors serve as preparation, complete with Travel Companions, Roamers, and Mixers appearing.<br/>Items are unidentified except for incenses - It's common to not find a second copy of any given item.<br/>Overall, it's not too hard if you secure Travel Companions and spend time farming items and synthesizing,<br/>but you'll want to have a good understanding of each boss's characteristics to stand the best chance.

# Strategy

### General Advice

#### Preparation (1-20F)

Travel Companions and Roamers appear, so explore most floors, including making Behemoth Gates vanish.<br/>Generally speaking, 1-13F is the time to collect items and synthesis ingredients, then 14-20F is for synthesis,<br/>but Mixer is on the rare side despite spawning on 14-20F, so keep your synthesis expectations in check.<br/>Be sure to collect arrows between 8-10F in case you find the extremely powerful Rapid-fire Bracelet.<br/>Have Travel Companions hunt Thiefwalrus monsters for guaranteed item drops between 8-13F.<br/>Lastly, 15-20F have dark hallways, so some players like to hurry past these floors.

#### Boss Rush (21-24F)

The order of bosses is Jakaku → Madara → Embodiment of Batsu → Embodiment of Suiryu.<br/>Keep in mind that all non-boss monsters vanish from the current floor when a boss is defeated.<br/>Traps can still be generated on these floors, so it's best to avoid walking around too much.<br/>Expulsion Scroll can be used to wipe out all non-boss monsters, but it's ineffective against bosses.<br/>Likewise, Eradication Scroll doesn't make a boss vanish, and Sanctuary Scroll is nullified.

### Important Items

The value of items differs a bit from other dungeons thanks to the boss fights at the end.

#### Main Weapon

Prioritize a higher base value instead of an innate ability when it comes to choosing a main weapon.

#### Weapon Runes

Madara is a Normal type, and none of the bosses share weaknesses, so type-effective runes are a low priority.<br/>When it comes to synthesis, focus on effects that apply to a wide variety of foes instead of a single family,<br/>including Critical, Voracious Hit, Costly Hit, Hunger Power, and status inflicting runes.

#### Bracelets

There aren't many bracelets that help with combat in this dungeon, so bracelets aren't a big focus.<br/>Monster-find Bracelet and Item-find Bracelet can be found, but think of those as just a bonus.

<p><b><u>Rapid-fire Bracelet</u></b><br/>Increases projectile accuracy (84% → 91%) and lets you shoot 2 projectiles during the same turn.<br/>Arrows can be found on the ground, or collected from Bowboy family monsters and traps.</p>

<p><b><u>Monstercall Bracelet</u></b><br/>Doubles the speed at which monsters naturally spawn while equipped (ex: 30 turns → 15 turns).<br/>Can be utilized to encounter more Thiefwalruses (8-13F) and Mixers (14-20F).</p>

<p><b><u>Affixing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Keeps your equipment safe from Swordsman (18-20F)'s special attack.<br/>Travel Companions following behind you always dodge parried equipment, so it's not essential.</p>

#### Projectiles

<p><b><u>Iron Arrow</u></b><br/>Can be shot at Madara to change the active incense effect to Evasive Incense.<br/>If you also have Poison Arrows, it's fine to use up Iron Arrows during the Jakaku boss fight.</p>

<p><b><u>Poison Arrow</u></b><br/>Shoot these at Nigiri Morph if you want to perform Nigiri Morph Factory and activate Sumo status.<br/>Otherwise, Jakaku gains attack power and Batsu is immune, so use them against other enemies.</p>

#### Grass / Food

Avoid eating unidentified grasses unless you find 2 copies to prevent wasted uses.

<p><b><u>Power-up Grass, Swift Grass, Invincible Grass</u></b><br/>Either eat these yourself or throw them at Travel Companions during a boss fight.</p>

<p><b><u>Bellyexpand Seed</u></b><br/>Throw it at a Mixer to increase the number of items the Mixer can swallow and synthesize by 1.</p>

<p><b><u>Antidote Grass</u></b><br/>Eat it after fighting Jakaku to restore lost strength, but unnecessary if you have a Rejuvenation Pot.<br/>Can also be thrown at Jakaku or other Draining type monsters to deal 50 damage.</p>

<p><b><u>Sweet Potato</u></b><br/>The effect is pointless during a boss fight, so use it during the dungeon portion.<br/>However, keep in mind that Travel Companions will also warp to a different room.</p>

#### Scrolls

Similar to grasses, avoid reading unidentified scrolls unless you find 2 copies to prevent wasted uses.<br/>Scrolls can get wet during the Embodiment of Suiryu boss fight on 24F, unless you have a Spearscale Shield.

<p><b><u>Confusion Scroll, Fixer Scroll, Slumber Scroll, Jitters Scroll</u></b><br/>Helpful against regular monsters during boss fights, but Slumber Scroll has a drawback.<br/>All 4 of these scrolls have a buying price of 300 Gitan, and a selling price of 120 Gitan.</p>

<p><b><u>Sanctuary Scroll</u></b><br/><span class="redText">Placing this scroll on the ground does nothing during a boss fight.</span><br/>Use it during the dungeon portion - Don't try to save it for any of the boss floors.</p>

<p><b><u>Blank Scroll</u></b><br/>Bosses are immune to both thrown Eradication Scroll and Expulsion Scroll, so it's not an insta-win option.<br/>However, Expulsion Scroll is effective against regular monsters even on boss floors.</p>

#### Staves

Disguising Staff and Sealing Staff are the 2 most important staves for boss fights.<br/>Madara and Batsu both have trickier specials, so Sealing Staff should be saved for those 2 in particular.<br/>Other staves can be useful against bosses, but single-target effects might be too slow to out-pace added foes.

<p><b><u>Disguising Staff</u></b><br/>Each use basically ensures 8 turns of safety for you and your Travel Companions.<br/>If you identify it, do everything you can to save it for boss floors instead of using it earlier.</p>

<p><b><u>Sealing Staff</u></b><br/>Swing it at a boss at the start of a fight to shut down special attacks and monster summons.<br/>If you only have 2 uses left, save it for Madara and Batsu (Batsu is immune to thrown staves).</p>

<p><b><u>Empathetic Staff</u></b><br/>If you're planning on trading hits with a boss, swing this beforehand to deal some extra damage.</p>

#### Incenses

<p><b><u>Heat-ban Incense</u></b><br/>Light this during the Embodiment of Batsu fight to receive 0 damage from the boss's attacks.</p>

<p><b><u>Blurry Incense</u></b><br/>Light this and shoot arrows at the boss while it mostly helplessly roams around the room.<br/>However, it can make it tricky to work with Travel Companions or track creatures in general.</p>

<p><b><u>Aggressive Incense</u></b><br/>Useful if you want to try and rush down the boss with Travel Companions or such.<br/>You'll want to use an item like Disguising Staff or Confusion Scroll beforehand.</p>

<p><b><u>Cautious Incense</u></b><br/>Keeps your items safe against Thiefwalrus and Swordsman special attacks.</p>

#### Pots

<p><b><u>Preservation Pot</u></b><br/>Lets you carry more items for boss floors, synthesis ingredients, etc.<br/>Insert important scrolls into Preservation Pots before the Embodiment of Suiryu fight to keep them dry.</p>

<p><b><u>Hilarious Pot</u></b><br/>Effectively shuts down enemies for a while on a boss floor.</p>

<p><b><u>Water Gun Pot</u></b><br/>Use it against Embodiment of Batsu (or Megataur) to cripple its attack power.</p>

<p><b><u>Identifier Pot</u></b><br/>Insert grasses or scrolls instead of bracelets - And ideally after narrowing down options by price.<br/>Grasses to aim for - 70/25 (Swift, Power-up, status inflicting) and 400/160 (Revival, Invincible).<br/>Scrolls to aim for - 300/120 (Confusion, Jitters, Slumber, Fixer).</p>

<p><b><u>Synthesis Pot</u></b><br/>Mixer is quite rare in this dungeon, so this pot can still be helpful.</p>

### Farming Techniques

#### Collecting Arrows (8-10F)

Bowboy (8-10F) provides Wooden Arrows, and Crossbowboy (9-10F) provides Iron Arrows.<br/>The dungeon portion can be tough, so it doesn't hurt to collect 99 arrows even if you lack a Rapid-fire Bracelet.<br/>Options include Earthmound Staff, Evasive Incense, Earth Ninja D (8F), Bored Kappa (9-10F), and rocks + traps.

#### Thiefwalrus Hunting (8-13F)

Thiefwalrus appears beween 8-13F, so just linger and have Travel Companions defeat them risk-free.<br/>Porky the Pirate can throw rocks at the Thiefwalrus and make it warp if the rock misses, so he's not reliable.

#### Nigiri Morph Factory (13-15F)

The Lv2 Nigiri Morph appears between 13-15F, so it's technically possible to have it create Large Onigiri.<br/>Unlucky Staff hasn't been sighted, so use Poison Arrows or Poison Grass to lower Nigiri Morph's attack power.<br/>Travel Companions and Skull Mage (13-14F) complicate things, and you only have a max of 1200 turns on a floor,<br/>so it's quite debatable if it's even worth the trouble in this dungeon.

# Floor Guide

### General Outline

Explore for items, Travel Companions, Roamers, and shops, and make Behemoth Gates vanish for extra items.<br/>1-13F is the floor range where you're searching for synthesis ingredients and useful items for boss fights,<br/>14-20F is the floor range where you synthesize and finalize your item selection, and 21-24F is the boss rush.

<p><span class="highlightYellow">Notable:</span> Pit Mamel (1-2F), Spearfish (1-3F), Pullfrog (1-3F), Death Reaper (2-3F), Firepuff (2-4F)</p>

Nothing should be much of a threat on these floors unless you immediately get surrounded.<br/>Spearfish might drop a Spearscale Shield if you're lucky, but don't get too fixated on trying for one.

### 21F - Jakaku (777 HP)

<p><span class="highlightYellow">Notable:</span> <span class="redText">Jakaku</span>, Soul Reaper, Dragon, Vile Scorpion, Super Gazer, Polygon Shaka, Floppy Hani, Muddy</p>

<div class="dungeonPageImage">
  <img src="../images/areas/other/jakaku.jpg"/>
</div>

<p><span class="highlightOrange2">Jakaku Battle</span> - The boss's stats are the same as the story battle, but the monster table differs.<br/>Hidden traps can be generated on the ground on boss floors, so avoid walking around if possible.<br/>Jakaku gains attack power when hit by Poison Arrows or Poison Grass, so never use those against it.<br/>Disguising Staff isn't effective if Floppy Hani is present, since a decoy leveling down undoes the disguise.</p>

Jakaku is arguably the hardest boss fight, provided you prepared counters for Madara and Batsu,<br/>
so go ahead and use some items that you don't plan on using for Madara and Embodiment of Batsu.<br/>Don't be afraid of losing a Travel Companion or two to power through this boss fight if needed, too.<br/>Polygon Shakas often warp in front of you and block your line of sight to Jakaku at the start.

1 new monster is naturally generated every 15 turns on 21F while the boss is present on the floor.<br/>All regular monsters vanish from the current floor when a boss is defeated, and the stairs becomes usable.<br/>A number of items are also generated on the ground, so pick them up before advancing floors.

#### Jakaku's Special Attacks

- Spit Poison (adjacent)
    - Lowers Shiren's strength by 1 and inflicts Slow status.
- Shove (adjacent)
    - Pushes Shiren back 1 tile, and makes him drop some items.
- Eat Monster (adjacent)
    - Jakaku eats an adjacent monster, restoring 30 HP and curing bad status conditions.

### 22F - Madara (666 HP)

<p><span class="highlightYellow">Notable:</span> <span class="redText">Madara</span>, Lv2 Ninjas (Rank C), Lv3 Ninjas (Rank B)</p>

<div class="dungeonPageImage">
  <img src="../images/areas/other/madara.jpg"/>
</div>

<p><span class="highlightOrange2">Madara Battle</span> - The fight starts with the player surrounded, so use a crowd-control item like Disguising Staff.<br/>Sealing Staff prevents Madara from using special attacks, including summoning additional monsters.<br/>Another option is to throw an item like Sedating Grass to temporarily prevent Madara from acting.</p>

Madara's <span class="pinkText">Breathe Smoke</span> special attack activates a floor-wide incense effect based on player actions.<br/>The effect can be overwritten by lighting your own incense, or by shooting an arrow or swinging a staff at Madara.<br/>Keep in mind that Madara has other specials like <span class="pinkText">Jump</span> and <span class="pinkText">Smoke Bomb</span> that let him warp to a different spot,<br/>so you might not always have a clear line of sight to Madara for arrows and staves as the fight progresses.<br/>If you run out of crowd-control items, expect to quickly burn through other items or lose Travel Companions.<br/>Also, Madara doesn't seem to react to Travel Companion attacks by using <span class="pinkText">Breathe Smoke</span> very much.

Regular monsters never naturally generate here, even while the boss is present on the floor.<br/>A number of items are also generated on the ground like before, but Fire Ninjas often destroy them.<br/>After slaying Madara, prepare an item that can be burned to light Heat-ban Incense before advancing floors,<br/>but Embodiment of Batsu can still be cleared without a Heat-ban Incense, so don't worry if you don't have one.

#### Madara's Special Attacks

- Breathe Smoke - Activates an incense effect based on the player's actions.
    - Evasive - Player deals damage to Madara with an arrow.
    - Defensive - Player uses an Aggressive Incense.
    - Reflective - Player uses a staff or scroll against Madara.
- Jump - Madara leaps away from the player.
    - He seems to use this special more often when his HP gets low.
- Smoke Bomb - Madara creates a clone (Roughly 60 HP?).
    - He seems to use this special more often when his HP gets low.
    - 2\~3 arrows should be enough to defeat the clone.
- Howl - Summons 3 ninjas from the floor's monster table.

### 23F - Embodiment of Batsu (888 HP)

<p><span class="highlightYellow">Notable:</span> <span class="redText">Batsu</span>, Tiger Hurler, Blazeous, Concusschin, Porkon, Megataur, Fluffy Bunny</p>

<div class="dungeonPageImage">
  <img src="../images/areas/other/batsu_2.jpg"/>
</div>

<p><span class="highlightOrange2">Batsu Battle</span> - Embodiment of Batsu burns projectiles aimed at him unless they have piercing properties.<br/>The boss can use <span class="pinkText">Howl</span> right at the start, so use a Sealing Staff or Confusion Scroll immediately.<br/>Fluffy Bunny can be problematic if summoned by <span class="pinkText">Howl</span> - Its special heals a different enemy's HP by 50.<br/>Batsu is likely the toughest boss, but Heat-ban Incense or Water Gun Pot instantly cripples its damage output.</p>

While there are ways to make this boss fight trivial, expect a tough fight if you lack those options.<br/>The monster table includes many strong foes, and Batsu himself has high attack power and a wider range.<br/>Embodiment of Batsu's charge-up attack can be interrupted by inflicting Paralyzed status.<br/>If you're out of useful items, you might actually be out of luck at this point.

#### Embodiment of Batsu's Special Attacks

- Flame Attack (2 tiles ahead)
    - Deals 20\~30 damage when you have a shield with 30 defense.
- Charge Attack (3 tile radius)
    - Cures bad status conditions including Weakened status, then deals 80\~100 damage.
    - This attack counts as explosion damage, so Blast Shield halves damage received.
- Sandstorm (anywhere)
    - Creates sand pillars on the field.
        - The boss can walk through these pillars while destroying them.
- Howl (anywhere)
    - Summons 3 monsters from the floor's monster table.
