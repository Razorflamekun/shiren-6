# Strategy

<h3 id="general-advice-(first-clear-&-sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Yellow">General Advice (First Clear & Sacred Tree Extension)</h3>

<h4 id="trapper-status" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/icons/21.png"/> Trapper Status</h4>

Shiren's color palette changes to a purple one while he has Trapper status.<br/>All hidden traps are revealed, and traps can be picked up, thrown, or set on the ground.<br/>Monsters can be ensnared by traps, and traps never activate when Shiren walks on top of them.<br/>Once set, traps can't be picked up again, and the status is lost while the bracelet is unequipped.

Shiren earns 10% of the regular amount of experience points when a monster steps on a trap,<br/>and also earns x2.0 experience points when a monster is defeated by a trap that deals damage.<br/>However, traps like Rotting Trap that instantly defeat a monster do not grant experience points.

<h4 id="trapping-floating-types" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/monsters_s/5-2.png"/> Trapping Floating Type Monsters</h4>

Floating types normally can't be ensnared by traps since they float above the trap on the ground.<br/>However, you can still lure them onto a trap → shoot arrows until one misses to activate the trap,<br/>resulting in the Floating type monster getting caught in the trap located underneath them.

<p><u>Floating Type Monsters</u>:</p>

|Monster|Floor|
|-|-|
|Pumphantasm|4-5F|
|Hell Reaper|7-9F|
|Flamepuff|18-20F|
|Soul Reaper|40F, 54-60F, 64-70F, 75-79F, 85-89F, 95-99F|
|Pumplich|49-53F, 60-63F, 70-74F, 80-84F, 90-94F|

<h4 id="floor-dragon-family-warning" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/monsters_s/35-1.png"/> Floor Dragon Family Warning</h4>

Floor Dragon monsters can't be ensnared by traps at all, so don't try to trap them.<br/>Dragonkiller, Dragon Shield, and arrows are a significant help against this monster family.

<p><u>Floor Dragon Monsters</u>:</p>

|Monster|Floor|
|-|-|
|Floor Dragon|13-15F|
|Dragon Head|30-39F|
|Dragon Pit|93-99F|

<h4 id="trap-based-explosion-warning" class="heading4Yellow"><img src="../images/traps/6.png"/> Trap-Based Explosion Warning</h4>

Blast Trap and Explosion Trap function differently under Trapper status against monsters:

<div class="trapIcon">
<ul>
  <li><img src="../images/traps/6.png"/> <u>Blast Trap</u>
    <ul>
      <li>Normal - Monsters caught in the blast are instantly defeated.</li>
      <li>Trapper - Reduces the monster's current HP by 50%.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li><img src="../images/traps/7.png"/> <u>Explosion Trap</u>
    <ul>
      <li>Normal - Monsters caught in the blast are instantly defeated.</li>
      <li>Trapper - Reduces the monster's current HP to 1.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>
</div>

<h3 id="trap-notes" class="heading3">Trap Notes (First Clear & Sacred Tree Extension)</h3>

<h4 id="rock-/-boulder-/-log-/-tripping" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/4.png"/> Rock <img src="../images/traps/5.png"/> Boulder <img src="../images/traps/9.png"/> Log <img src="../images/traps/1.png"/> Tripping</h4>

<p><u>Rock Trap</u> deals 16~20 damage, and <u>Boulder Trap</u> deals 32~38 damage.<br/>These traps can be used to reliably earn experience points if placed in intersections.<br/><u>Log Trap</u> and <u>Tripping Trap</u> can also be used to deal damage, but they're not as powerful.</p>

<h4 id="spinning-/-sleep-/-blinding-/-sluggish-/-delusion-/-onigiri" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/11.png"/> Spinning <img src="../images/traps/12.png"/> Sleep <img src="../images/traps/14.png"/> Blinding <img src="../images/traps/18.png"/> Sluggish <img src="../images/traps/15.png"/> Delusion <img src="../images/traps/16.png"/> Onigiri</h4>

These are traps that inflict a negative stauts condition, with <u>Delusion Trap</u> being the best.<br/>Monsters with Deluded status won't attack Shiren, and will instead run away from him forever.<br/>The second best trap is <u>Blinding Trap</u>, since its effect similarly never wears off for monsters.<br/> <u>Sleep Trap</u> lasts 6 turns and <u>Onigiri Trap</u> lasts 10 turns, so use those on foes you can slay quickly.<br/><u>Spinning Trap</u> lasts 20 turns and <u>Sluggish Trap</u> lasts forever, making them good stalling options.

<h4 id="curse-/-silver-seal" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/22.png"/> Curse <img src="../images/traps/23.png"/> Silver-seal</h4>

<div class="trapIcon">
  <p>Inflicts a monster with Sealed status, making them unable to use abilities.<br/>Nice when hunting Thiefwalrus (5-13F), though you might want to pair it with a stat-lowering trap,<br/>such as <img src="../images/traps/19.png"/> <u>Removal Trap</u>, <img src="../images/traps/10.png"/> <u>Hunger Trap</u>, or <img src="../images/traps/16.png"/> <u>Onigiri Trap</u> (<img src="../images/traps/20.png"/> <u>Rust Trap</u> heals Aquatic types).<br/>Also great for disabling Skull Wizard (19-20F), Super Gazer (22-24F), Vile Scorpion (32-86F), etc.</p>
</div>

<h4 id="rust" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/20.png"/> Rust</h4>

Reduces defense + inflicts a similar effect to getting hit by a Water Gun Pot.<br/>Basically makes Masked Samurai (10-12F), Minotaur (16-20F), and Pop Tank (16-18F) a non-issue.<br/>However, this trap cannot be used to transform enemies with Onigiri status into Rotten Onigiri.<br/>Mudkin (11-14F) and other Aquatic type monsters will have their HP restored by 25% instead.

<h4 id="removal" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/19.png"/> Removal</h4>

Reduces a monster's attack power and defense - <span class="orangeText2">Does not inflict Sealed status!</span><br/>Intentionally step on it yourself if you equip a cursed weapon, shield, or bracelet.

<h4 id="blast-/-explosion" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/6.png"/> Blast <img src="../images/traps/7.png"/> Explosion</h4>

<div class="trapIcon">
  <p>Under Trapper status, HP is reduced by 50% for <u>Blast Trap</u>, and HP is set to 1 for <u>Explosion Trap</u>.<br/><span class="orangeText2">Always unequip the Trapper Bracelet before stepping on it when you want to one-shot monsters.</span><br/>Otherwise, you're extremely likely to collapse if you step on it while you're surrounded by foes.<br/>Use it after luring a monster onto an <img src="../images/traps/16.png"/> <u>Onigiri Trap</u> to instantly turn them into Grilled Onigiri.<br/>Overall, prioritize carrying stronger traps like <img src="../images/traps/21.png"/> <u>Rotting Trap</u> or <img src="../images/traps/15.png"/> <u>Delusion Trap</u>.</p>
</div>

<h4 id="rotting" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/21.png"/> Rotting</h4>

<div class="trapIcon">
  <p>Monsters who step on this trap will be instantly defeated and transformed into Rotten Onigiri.<br/>Rotten Onigiri can be thrown at a foe to deal 5 damage + inflict a negative status condition,<br/>which is randomly selected from Weakened, Confused, Asleep, Blind, or Deluded status.<br/><img src="../images/traps/6.png"/> <u>Blast Trap</u> or <img src="../images/traps/7.png"/> <u>Explosion Trap</u> can be used to turn Rotten Onigiri into Grilled Onigiri.</p>
</div>

<h4 id="trap-setting" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/31.png"/> Trap-setting</h4>

Step on this to increase the number of traps on the ground.<br/>Save it for when the number of traps in your inventory starts to get low.

<h4 id="spring" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/8.png"/> Spring</h4>

Step on this when you're being chased to warp to a different location on the current floor.<br/>Similar to Leaping Grass, but it can also be used to warp monsters instead of yourself.

<h4 id="wooden-arrow-/-poison-arrow" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/2.png"/> Wooden Arrow <img src="../images/traps/3.png"/> Poison Arrow</h4>

Use these traps to stock up on arrows instead of setting them for enemies to step on.<br/>Place it down alongside a wall, then throw an item at the wall so that the item lands on the trap.<br/>Poison Arrows are fantastic in particular, since 1 arrow reduces enemy attack power by \~50%,<br/>but don't shoot them at Scorpion (4-7F), Ghost Radish (16-17F), Daze Radish (26-29F),<br/>or Vile Scorpion (32-86F), since it results in an attack power boost via Empowered status.

<h4 id="monsterizing-/-summoning-/-rage" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/27.png"/> Monsterizing <img src="../images/traps/24.png"/> Summoning <img src="../images/traps/26.png"/> Rage</h4>

<p><span class="redText">Negative effect traps</span> - These 3 offer nearly zero advantages for the player when activated.<br/><u>Monsterizing Trap</u> and <u>Summoning Trap</u> have a use for item drops, but <u>Rage Trap</u> is useless.<br/>Move these to a spot where foes won't step on them, or insert them into Transmutation Pots.</p>

<h4 id="pitfall" class="heading4"><img src="../images/traps/28.png"/> Pitfall</h4>

Emergency escape option that can also be used to safely steal from a dungeon shop.<br/>To utilize it during Sumo status, set it underfoot next to a wall → shoot an arrow at the wall.

<h3 id="important-items-(first-clear)" class="heading3Purple">Important Items (First Clear)</h3>

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/weapon/4.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/4.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>High Base Value Gear</u></b><br/>Mixers don't appear, so prioritize high base stats instead of special abilities.<br/>Hell Reaper (7-9F) floors are tough without a shield in particular.</p>

<p><b><u>Dragonkiller, Dragon Shield</u></b><br/>Nice against Floor Dragon (13-15F) and Flamepuff (18-20F), who don't step on traps.<br/>Equipping both and activating the resonance effect lets you potentially one-shot Floor Dragon.</p>

<p><b><u>Walrus Stopper, Primal Axe, Steel Severer</u></b><br/>Walrus Stopper is nice as a secondary shield to help with item collection up to 12F or so.<br/>The latter 2 weapons help against Beast and Metal types that appear near the end.</p>

<p><b><u>Watchful Shield, Counter Shield</u></b><br/>Most damage received from monsters is through normal attacks, making these shields great.</p>

<h4 id="bracelets-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/bracelet.png"/> Bracelets</h4>

<p><b><u>Trapper's Bracelet</u></b><br/>The player starts with this item, but additional copies can be found inside the dungeon.<br/>Strength Grass is on the uncommon side, so don't plan on being able to equip 2 bracelets.<br/>It's a good idea to keep this bracelet equipped to avoid accidentally losing it.</p>

<h4 id="projectiles-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/arrow.png"/> Projectiles</h4>

<p><b><u>Poison Arrow, Wooden Arrow</u></b><br/>A single Poison Arrow reduces enemy attack power by ~50%.<br/>Collect arrows from any arrow traps you encounter, repositioning the trap as needed.<br/>Intentionally step on a <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/17.png"/></span> <u>Far-throwing Trap</u> to give your arrows piercing properties.<br/>Don't shoot Poison Arrows at Scorpion (4-7F) or Ghost Radish (16-17F).</p>

<h4 id="staves-&-scrolls-&-incenses-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> <img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> <img src="../images/other/incense.png"/> Staves & Scrolls & Incenses</h4>

<p><b><u>Mapping Scroll, Guiding Staff</u></b><br/><span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/29.png"/></span> <u>Floor-exiting Trap</u> and <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/28.png"/></span> <u>Pitfall Trap</u> are never naturally generated in this dungeon,<br/>so Mapping Scroll and Guiding Staff are the next best options for quickly advancing floors.</p>

<p><b><u>Disguising Staff</u></b><br/>Can be used to create a higher level monster early on for experience points,<br/>and also a fantastic crowd control option on later floors when surrounded by monsters.<br/>Feel free to carry 2 or more copies of this staff, as it's extremely useful.</p>

<p><b><u>Vaulting Staff, Switching Staff, Blurry Incense</u></b><br/>These items are nice to have on hand in case you encounter a single room Monster House.</p>

<p><b><u>Trap Scroll</u></b><br/>Similar to <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/31.png"/></span> <u>Trap-setting Trap</u>, use it when the number of traps in your inventory gets low.<br/>Consider your current Fullness and remaining food items before reading this scroll.</p>

<h4 id="pots-(first-clear)" class="heading4Purple"><img src="../images/other/pot.png"/> Pots</h4>

<p><b><u>Preservation Pot</u></b><br/>Lets you carry more traps and consumable items, which is incredibly handy.<br/>Exorcism Scrolls should be saved for cursed Preservation Pots you find on the ground.<br/>If you encounter a Bouncy Wall Room, see if you can duplicate Preservation Pots first.</p>

<p><b><u>Bottomless Pot</u></b><br/>Break a Bottomless Pot to generate a number of <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/28.png"/></span> <u>Pitfall Traps</u> equal to the pot's capacity.<br/>This dungeon has descending stairs, so said traps can be used to advance to the next floor.<br/>Particularly great when attempting a speedrun of this dungeon.</p>

<h3 id="important-items-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Blue">Important Items (Sacred Tree Extension)</h3>

<h4 id="weapon-&-shield-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/weapon/15.png"/> <img src="../images/shield/10.png"/> Weapon & Shield</h4>

<p><b><u>Dragonkiller</u></b><br/>Nice against Sky Dragon (28-99F), Dragon Head (30-39F), Dragon Pit (93-99F), etc.<br/>The best main weapon for this dungeon, and even better when paired with Dragon Shield,<br/>since the Anti-Dragon damage multiplier receives a boost (x1.5 → x2.5).</p>

<p><b><u>Drain Slayer</u></b><br/>Great against Polygon Shaka (30-99F) and Vile Scorpion (32-86F) in particular.<br/>Not exactly essential if you find a Cleansing Bracelet, though.</p>

<p><b><u>Sickle of Salvation, Sky Sword</u></b><br/>Helpful against Floating types that can't be trapped like Soul Reaper and Pumplich.<br/>However, Dragonkiller or Drain Slayer should probably be prioritized above these options.<br/>Soul Reaper appears on 40F, 54-60F, 64-70F, 75-79F, 85-89F, and 95-99F.<br/>Pumplich appears on 49-53F, 60-63F, 70-74F, 80-84F, and 90-94F.</p>

<p><b><u>Kama Itachi</u></b><br/>Weak, so only viable when combined with Sumo status + Monster-find Bracelet.</p>

<p><b><u>Axe of the Minotaur</u></b><br/>Occasionally dropped by Minotaur (16-20F), but don't expect it to drop even if you linger.<br/>Chance-based rune, but effective at boosting your damage dealt against any monster.<br/>Synthesis isn't possible in this dungeon, so you can't transfer the rune.</p>

<p><b><u>Shield of Sating</u></b><br/>Mostly for players who like to maintain Sumo status throughout the dungeon.</p>

<p><b><u>Dragon Shield, Kappa's Dish</u></b><br/>Sky Dragon (28-99F) and Baby Tank (29-99F) can be a menace in single room Monster Houses,<br/>so these are better main shield options for those who don't plan on maintaining Sumo status.</p>

<h4 id="bracelets-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/bracelet.png"/> Bracelets</h4>

<p><b><u>Rapid-fire Bracelet</u></b><br/>Doubles the power of projectiles, letting you slay Vile Scorpion (32-86F) before it gets close.<br/>Hallways remain visible through 50F, and Baby Tank (29-99F) can be used to restock arrows.<br/>For 51-99F, either shoot arrows to check for incoming foes or use Monster-find Bracelet.</p>

<p><b><u>Monster-find Bracelet</u></b><br/>Counters dark hallways (51-99F), and makes it easier to deal with Soul Reaper and Pumplich.<br/>Also prevents getting surprised by Vile Scorpion (32-86F)'s special attack in dark hallways.<br/>Soul Reaper appears on 40F, 54-60F, 64-70F, 75-79F, 85-89F, and 95-99F.<br/>Pumplich appears on 49-53F, 60-63F, 70-74F, 80-84F, and 90-94F.</p>

<p><b><u>Cleansing Bracelet</u></b><br/>Nullifies Vile Scorpion (32-86F)'s special attack - it's fine to discard it once you reach 87F.<br/>Can also be used to block the Strength -3 effect when eating Rotten Onigiri.</p>

<h4 id="projectiles-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/arrow.png"/> Projectiles</h4>

<p><b><u>Wooden Arrow</u></b><br/>Obtained from <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/2.png"/></span> <u>Wooden Arrow Trap</u> or Bowboy (6-9F).<br/>Mainly used before collecting Iron Arrows from Crossbowboy (10-12F) or Baby Tank (29-99F).</p>

<p><b><u>Iron Arrow</u></b><br/>Obtained from Crossbowboy (10-12F) or Baby Tank (29-99F).<br/>Earthmound Staff or Evasive Incense makes it trivial to collect arrows from Baby Tank.<br/>Feel free to collect literally hundreds of arrows if you find a Rapid-fire Bracelet.</p>

<p><b><u>Poison Arrow</u></b><br/>Obtained from <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/3.png"/></span> <u>Poison Arrow Trap</u> - 1 arrow reduces enemy attack power by ~50%.<br/>Excellent against stronger foes like Sky Dragon, Baby Tank, and Soul Reaper in particular.<br/>Never shoot arrows at Hoppin' Slugger (37-89F) since it has a 50% chance to reflect them.<br/>Don't shoot Poison Arrows at Daze Radish (26-29F) or Vile Scorpion (32-86F).</p>

<h4 id="staves-&-scrolls-&-grass-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/staff.png"/> <img src="../images/other/scroll.png"/> <img src="../images/other/grass.png"/> Staves & Scrolls & Grass</h4>

<p><b><u>Paralyzing Staff, Narrow-escape Staff, Sealing Staff, Sluggish Staff</u></b><br/>Single-target options for disabling enemy special attacks or creating distance.<br/>Sealing Staff can backfire if it hits a Baby Tank (29-99F) or Hoppin' Slugger (37-89F).<br/>Swing Narrow-escape Staff at a Hoppin' Slugger for a 50% chance to be warped to the stairs.</p>

<p><b><u>Skull Mage's Staff</u></b><br/>Obtained from Skull Mage (22-23F, 40-59F) or Skull Wizard (19-20F).<br/>Single-target option for either shutting down or warping a monster elsewhere.</p>

<p><b><u>Switching Staff, Vaulting Staff, Leaping Grass</u></b><br/>Emergency escape options for Monster Houses, but can't be used during Sumo status.<br/>Staves are best for single room MHs, while Leaping Grass is best for pop-up style MHs.<br/><span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/8.png"/></span> <u>Spring Trap</u> can be used like Leaping Grass, but foes can also step on the trap.<br/>If you're maintaining Sumo status, prioritize scrolls instead of these items.</p>

<p><b><u>Earthmound Staff</u></b><br/>A single use lets you collect hundreds of Iron Arrows from Baby Tank (29-99F).<br/>Can also be used to create a safe spot where you shoot arrows at foes through corners.</p>

<p><b><u>Blank Scroll</u></b><br/>It might be best to save this scroll as an Expulsion Scroll for a single room Monster House.<br/>If writing Eradication Scroll, it's probably best to throw it at either N'dama or Soul Reaper.<br/>Soul Reaper appears on 40F, 54-60F, 64-70F, 75-79F, 85-89F, and 95-99F.<br/>N'dama appears on 60-62F, 69-71F, 78-80F, and 97-99F.</p>

<p><b><u>Confusion Scroll, Slumber Scroll, Jitters Scroll</u></b><br/>Sweet Potato can't be found in this dungeon, so these are handy for Monster Houses.</p>

<h4 id="food-&-pots-&-incenses-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading4Blue"><img src="../images/other/food.png"/> <img src="../images/other/pot.png"/> <img src="../images/other/incense.png"/> Food & Pots & Incenses</h4>

<p><b><u>Grilled Onigiri</u></b><br/>Main food source, since Nigiri Morph family monsters don't appear in this dungeon.<br/><span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/21.png"/></span> <u>Rotting Trap</u> for Rotten Onigiri → <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/6.png"/></span> <u>Blast Trap</u> or get hit by Sky Dragon (28-99F)'s fire.<br/>Grilled Onigiri increases Max Fullness by 2 when eaten when Fullness is already full,<br/>so prepare to activate Sumo status for the final stretch with Popdillo (90-99F).</p>

<p><b><u>Identifier Pot</u></b><br/>Insert bracelets in search of Rapid-fire, Monster-find, and Cleansing bracelets.<br/>If you feel like your bracelet selection is good, use it as storage for Revival Grass.</p>

<p><b><u>Bottomless Pot</u></b><br/>Break a Bottomless Pot to generate a number of <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/28.png"/></span> <u>Pitfall Traps</u> equal to the pot's capacity.<br/>This dungeon has descending stairs, so said traps can be used to advance to the next floor.<br/>Save Pitfall Traps for single room Monster Houses or dangerous floor ranges.<br/>It goes without saying, but store Pitfall Traps in Preservation Pots.</p>

<p><b><u>Water Gun Pot</u></b><br/>Splash water on a Soul Reaper to reduce its attack power by 8 stages.<br/>Soul Reaper appears on 40F, 54-60F, 64-70F, 75-79F, 85-89F, and 95-99F.<br/>However, beware of accidentally making an important trap vanish.</p>

<p><b><u>Heat-ban Incense</u></b><br/>Counters Sky Dragon (28-99F)'s flames in a single room Monster House.</p>

<p><b><u>Evasive Incense</u></b><br/>Counters Baby Tank (29-99F)'s Iron Arrows in a single room Monster House.<br/>Your arrows also miss while it's active, so use a <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/28.png"/></span> <u>Pitfall Trap</u> after lighting it.</p>

<p><b><u>Blurry Incense</u></b><br/>Counters a single room Monster House no matter what your playstyle is like.<br/>This applies to all incenses, but it's best to select an arrow or trap when lighting it.</p>

<h3 id="farming-techniques" class="heading3Green">Farming Techniques</h3>

<h4 id="grilled-onigiri" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/other/food.png"/> Grilled Onigiri</h4>

<p><b><u>Rotting Trap Method</u></b>:<br/>Set a <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/21.png"/></span> <u>Rotting Trap</u> on the ground and lure enemies onto it to obtain Rotten Onigiri,<br/>then get hit by fire/blast damage or step on a <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/6.png"/></span> <u>Blast Trap</u> to turn them into Grilled Onigiri.<br/>Non-trap sources include Pop Tank (16-18F), Flamepuff (18-20F), Sky Dragon (28-99F), etc.</p>

<p><b><u>Onigiri Trap Method</u></b>:<br/>Set an <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/16.png"/></span> <u>Onigiri Trap</u> on the ground and lure a foe onto it to inflict Onigiri status,<br/>then lure the enemy onto a <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/6.png"/></span> <u>Blast Trap</u> to turn them into Grilled Onigiri.</p>

<h4 id="wooden-arrows,-poison-arrows" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/traps/2.png"/> <img src="../images/traps/3.png"/> Wooden Arrows / Poison Arrows</h4>

If you spot an arrow trap, place it next to a wall → throw items onto the trap to collect arrows.

<h4 id="cave-mamel-hunting-(1-3f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/1-3.png"/> Cave Mamel Hunting (1-3F)</h4>

Pit Mamel (1-3F) can be leveled up into a Cave Mamel and hunted for <span class="oceanBlueText">4,000+</span> experience points.<br/>Easy level up options include <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/14.png"/> <u>Blinding Trap</u></span>, Disguising Staff, Blinding grass, Jitters Scroll, etc.<br/>Set non-damaging traps (ex: <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/20.png"/></span> <u>Rust Trap</u>) for Cave Mamel to step on for <span class="oceanBlueText">200</span> exp per activation,<br/>then end with a trap that deals damage (ex: <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/4.png"/></span> <u>Rock Trap</u>) for <span class="oceanBlueText">4,000</span> experience points.

<h4 id="thiefwalrus-/-greenwalrus-hunting-(5-13f,-25-29f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/29-1.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/29-2.png"/> Thiefwalrus / Greenwalrus Hunting (5-13F, 25-29F)</h4>

Use traps to safely hunt Thiefwalrus (5-13F) or Greenwalrus (25-29F) for extra items.<br/>However, be careful of their higher attack power if you decide to inflict Sealed status.<br/>Thiefwalrus has <span class="orangeText">25</span> attack power, and Greenwalrus has <span class="orangeText">30</span> attack power.

<h4 id="iron-arrows-(10-12f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/15-2.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/17-1.png"/> Iron Arrows (10-12F)</h4>

Crossbowboy (10-12F) appears with Bored Kappa (10-12F), so use the combo to collect arrows.<br/>The exact setup varies, but the idea is to position a Bored Kappa between you and a Crossbowboy.<br/>You can hold ZR and press A to quickly pick up arrows instead of opening the menu every time.<br/>Picking up items doesn't regen HP, so don't forget to walk or step in place to heal as needed.

<h4 id="great-samurai-/-lordly-samurai-hunting-(10-12f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/9-3.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/9-4.png"/> Great Samurai / Lordly Samurai Hunting (10-12F)</h4>

Slay Masked Samurais (10-12F) → let Ghost Samurais level up a Masked Samurai to Lv3-4,<br/>then lure the Lv3-4 Masked Samurai onto a <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/20.png"/></span> <u>Rust Trap</u> to lower its attack power by 8 stages.<br/>You can safely trade hits at that point, though you might need to walk to regen HP at times.<br/>Great Samurai provides <span class="oceanBlueText">840</span> experience points, and Lordly Samurai <span class="oceanBlueText">1,280</span> experience points.<br/>Set non-damaging traps for more exp, and slay it with a damaging trap to double exp.

<h4 id="mudster-/-mudder-hunting-(11-12f)" class="heading4Green"><img src="../images/monsters/32-3.png"/> <img src="../images/monsters/32-4.png"/> Mudster / Mudder Hunting (11-12F)</h4>

Mudkin (11-14F) and Masked Samurai (10-12F) overlap, so hunt Lv3-4 Mudkins to level up.<br/>Crossbowboy (10-12F) often significantly interferes, so it's best to seal off the stairs room.<br/>As always, unequip your gear and stand on the stairs when punching Lv3-4 Mudkins for exp.<br/>Don't inflict Sealed status - Mudster has <span class="orangeText">35</span> attack power, and Mudder has <span class="orangeText">45</span> attack power.<br/>Mudster provides <span class="oceanBlueText">460</span> experience points, and Mudder <span class="oceanBlueText">920</span> experience points.

<h3 id="sacred-tree-extension" class="heading3Pink">Sacred Tree Extension</h3>

<h3 id="general-advice-(sacred-tree-extension)" class="heading3Red">General Advice</h3>

Focus on a balanced inventory of equipment, traps, movement related staves, and arrows.<br/>Keep an eye out for weapons and shields with special abilities, along with items that restore HP.<br/>Try to reach 10+ Max Strength so that you can equip 2 bracelets at the same time.

The monster table doesn't change much past 30F, food is plentiful, and Behemoths don't spawn.<br/>So feel free to start rushing stairs between 30-99F once your item selection looks good.<br/>Hallways are dark on 51-99F, so a stronger shield or Monster-find Bracelet helps a lot.

<h3 id="1-20f" class="heading3Red">1-20F</h3>

Perform [farming techniques](#farming-techniques) to level up to Lv20+ for the fastest HP regen speed,<br/>increase Max Fullness by creating Grilled Onigiri, identify items, explore for dungeon shops,<br/>hunt Thiefwalrus (5-13F) for item drops, and collect Iron Arrows from Crossbowboy (10-12F).<br/>It's fine to sell or discard gear that doesn't seem useful, but be sure to identify bracelets.<br/>Remember that Mixers and Synthesis Pots don't appear - <span class="pinkText">synthesis isn't possible</span>.

<h3 id="30-39f" class="heading3Red">30-39F</h3>

The long-term threats of the Sacred Tree Mode extension begin to appear one after the next.<br/>It begins with Sky Dragon (28-99F), Baby Tank (29-99F), and Polygon Shaka (30-99F),<br/>then continues with Vile Scorpion (32-86F) and Hoppin' Slugger (37-89F).<br/>Dragon Head (30-39F) also appears as a threat that can't be ensnared by traps at all.

Polygon Shaka and Dragon Head shouldn't be a notable problem compared to later threats,<br/>so do your best to defeat them while conserving inventory items.

<h3 id="40-59f" class="heading3Red">40-59F</h3>

Froggon (40-59F) and Skull Mage (40-59F) appear, and hallways are dark between 51-99F.<br/>The former always drops a high value Gitan bag, and the latter can drop Skull Mage's Staff.<br/>Gitan bags can be paired with <span class="trapIcon"><img src="../images/traps/17.png"/></span> <u>Far-throwing Trap</u> to help clear out a Monster House.<br/>Throw traps instead of shooting arrows when checking for incoming foes in hallways,<br/>since Hoppin' Slugger (37-89F) has a 50% chance to reflect arrows.

<p><span class="pinkText">Soul Reaper</span> and <span class="pinkText">Pumplich</span> are 2 Floating types that normally can't be ensnared by traps.<br/>Soul Reaper appears on 40F, 54-60F, 64-70F, 75-79F, 85-89F, and 95-99F.<br/>Pumplich appears on 49-53F, 60-63F, 70-74F, 80-84F, and 90-94F.<br/>Basically, these 2 monsters alternate every 5-7 floors.</p>

Soul Reaper is immune to traps, passes through walls, and has Swift 2 action speed.<br/>It's likely to strike first thanks to dark hallways, and deals heavy damage with a weak shield.<br/>Thankfully the initial enemy counts aren't as high as Heart of Serpentcoil Island or such,<br/>so stepping in place at the start and slaying initial spawns before exploring is effective.<br/>Throw an Eradication Scroll if you lack a good shield, Sumo status, or Monster-find Bracelet.
